# SEB-001-PRS-002 — Cellulose and Hemicellulose Product Requirements

**Revision:** 0.1 | **Date:** 2026-11-26 | **Status:** Draft requirements framework — customer validation required  
**Classification:** Preliminary product definition — not a process recipe or product guarantee

## 1. Purpose and scope
Define candidate products and qualification evidence for cellulose-rich solids and hemicellulose-derived streams recovered from hemp hurd fractionation. The product families are intentionally separated:
- Cellulose-rich solids, pulp or refined fiber.
- Hemicellulose-derived liquor, oligomers and purified C5 sugars (including xylose only where analytically confirmed).
- Downstream fermentation or chemical-conversion feedstocks, subject to customer and process validation.

A stream described as cellulose-rich or C5-rich is not automatically pure cellulose, purified xylose, food grade, fermentation grade or saleable product. Product identity and purity require validated analytical evidence.

## 2. Candidate product forms and customer interfaces
| Product form | Potential application | Required evidence before product claim |
|---|---|---|
| Cellulose-rich wet cake / pulp | Paper, molded fiber, composite reinforcement | Cellulose content, fiber morphology, residual lignin/hemicellulose, ash, moisture and end-use performance |
| Dried cellulose-rich material | Industrial cellulose derivatives or reinforcement feed | Purity, degree of polymerization where relevant, moisture, particle size, color, ash and contaminants |
| C5-rich process liquor | Refinery intermediate or fermentation feed | Sugar speciation, concentration, inhibitors, salts, residual reagents, microbial stability and variability |
| Purified xylose / sugar product | Chemical or fermentation feedstock | Identity, purity, moisture/solids, impurity profile, color, ash, microbial quality and customer method |
| Oligomer-rich stream | Enzymatic or chemical conversion intermediate | Oligomer distribution, degree of polymerization, monomer content and conversion behavior |

No market grade or application is selected in this revision. Customer discovery shall establish which product forms have a real specification and credible demand.

## 3. Feed and process traceability
Each lot shall link to incoming stalk and decorticated hurd IDs, feedstock assay, moisture basis, particle-size distribution, storage history, and process campaign. Record all water, acid/base, solvent, enzyme, neutralization chemicals, filter aids and other process inputs. Identify recycle, purge and cleaning streams separately.

For each process stage, record wet and dry mass, composition, pH where relevant, temperature, pressure, residence time, analytical method, sample ID and uncertainty. The mass balance shall include water addition during hydrolysis, reagent incorporation, dissolved organics, salts, off-gas, wastewater and retained material. Never infer product mass directly from polymer mass without accounting for reaction stoichiometry and losses.

## 6. Sampling and analytical quality
Develop separate sampling plans for solids, slurries and liquors. Homogenization and preservation must be appropriate to the sample; record filtration, dilution, storage temperature, holding time and any hydrolysis or derivatization performed before analysis. Use qualified laboratories and validated methods suitable for the matrix.

Report raw and calculated results, calibration/QC checks, replicate count, detection and quantitation limits where relevant, uncertainty, and dry/wet basis. Establish customer-approved methods before using analytical results as contractual release criteria.

## 7. Process and allocation safeguards
- Cellulose-rich solids sold as pulp/reinforcement are not simultaneously allocated to glucose production.
- Hemicellulose polymer mass is not equal to recovered xylose mass; hydrolysis adds water and may generate inhibitors or degradation products.
- Dissolved sugars, oligomers, salts and residual reagents must be distinguished in total dissolved solids.
- Recycle streams are internal flows, not new feed or additional product.
- Wastewater treatment and purge requirements must be included in process selection and cost accounting.
- Product purification, concentration and drying duties remain unselected until target customer specifications are known.

## 8. Packaging, storage and transport
Define wet cake, slurry, concentrate or dry powder form with each customer. Establish compatible containers, materials, moisture/temperature limits, microbial controls, preservation needs, shelf-life evidence, spill containment, labeling and transport classification. Do not assume a wet liquor is stable or that a dried product is safe from dust hazards.

## 9. Safety, environmental and regulatory review
Assess actual process chemistry for corrosivity, flammability, toxicity, pressure/temperature hazards, solvent exposure, incompatible materials, neutralization heat, and wastewater characteristics. Evaluate dust hazards for dried solids and worker exposure during sampling and packaging. Determine product-specific regulatory obligations from intended end use and destination; this draft makes no food, feed, pharmaceutical, cosmetic, food-contact or fermentation regulatory compliance claim.

## 10. Customer validation plan
1. Obtain written customer specifications for cellulose pulp, industrial cellulose or C5 feedstock.
2. Identify analytical methods and sample protocols accepted by each customer.
3. Run controlled bench/pilot campaigns using characterized feed lots and complete stream accounting.
4. Test product functionality in the intended downstream process, not only chemical composition.
5. Obtain customer feedback on impurity tolerance, concentration, moisture, packaging, consistency and volume.
6. Update the process route scorecard using evidence from the same feed lot and comparable accounting boundary.

## 11. Open items and release gate
- PRS-CH-01: Select customer-led product form(s) and intended use.
- PRS-CH-02: Obtain target specifications and analytical methods.
- PRS-CH-03: Establish carbohydrate assay and monomer/oligomer accounting.
- PRS-CH-04: Characterize inhibitors, salts, residual reagents and wastewater.
- PRS-CH-05: Validate downstream functionality and storage/transport requirements.

**Product release is not authorized** until identity, purity, measurable acceptance limits, analytical methods, sampling, stability, process capability and QA authority are established.
