# DEEP//RUN

A longer-form Arcade sublevel unlocked only by the canonical `arcade-singularity` artifact.

The run recombines learned movement ideas from five mastery cabinets into a five-stage descent: drift avoidance, lane gates, swarm geometry, pulse timing, and the white door. Direct URL access independently checks the artifact and remains sealed without it.

Clearing the descent grants `black-token`. Failure records `deep-run-loss`.
