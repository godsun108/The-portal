## Configuration and change control
1. Preserve the selected options and capacity ladder unless the owner approves a formal change.
2. Record every change to throughput, feedstock, process chemistry, product slate, site, operating hours, equipment duty, emissions basis, or product claims.
3. Update linked PFD, mass balance, equipment register, utilities, HSE, cost, and schedule documents together.
4. Maintain traceability from each design input to evidence or an explicit open-item number.
5. No purchase order, paid test, contract, site work, construction, commissioning, or operation is authorized by these documents.

## Design maturity and limitations
This is preliminary engineering / feasibility definition. It is not a process simulation, detailed design, equipment datasheet package, permit application, construction package, or operating procedure. Numeric yields, utility rates, emissions, equipment sizes, installed costs, and product revenues remain unverified unless explicitly stated otherwise.
