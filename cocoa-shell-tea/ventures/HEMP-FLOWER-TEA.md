# Hemp Flower / CBD Flower Herbal Tea — Feasibility Research
Date: 2026-10-12 | Status: RESEARCH ONLY, no sales, manufacturing, consumption trials or launch authority.

## Product vision
A tea/infusion made from cannabis/hemp botanical flower (potentially industrial hemp, low-cannabinoid hemp flower, or CBD-rich hemp flower), sold only through a verifiably lawful and safety-reviewed route. Separate venture concept from Cocoa Shell Tea. Do not assume the word "hemp" makes flower lawful as conventional food.

## Materially different product hypotheses
A. **Hemp-flower botanical infusion**, sold as conventional tea: food-use status, ingredient safety and cannabinoid exposure require specific review; FDA hemp-seed GRAS conclusions do NOT extend to leaves/flowers.
B. **CBD-rich hemp flower intended for brewing tea**: cannabinoid-containing ingestible product with complicated federal food restrictions, state-specific hemp extract definitions and labeling/age requirements. Avoid assuming a loose dried flower avoids the rules because CBD occurs naturally; the legal treatment of the exact product and marketing presentation needs qualified counsel.
C. **Permitted medical cannabis tea/infusion**: a different licensed medical cannabis channel, not ordinary grocery distribution and not currently authorized for this venture.
D. **Hemp-seed-based conventional beverage**: potentially simpler permitted food category, but hemp seed is nutritionally different and is not a botanical CBD flower tea; do not mislabel it.

## Current government guidance and unresolved questions
FDA: CBD/THC-added foods cannot be introduced into interstate commerce, and CBD/THC products are excluded from the dietary supplement definition under its published interpretation. The agency's no-questions GRAS conclusions cover specific hulled hemp seed, seed protein and seed oil inputs only, not cannabinoid-rich flower.
https://www.fda.gov/news-events/public-health-focus/fda-regulation-cannabis-and-cannabis-derived-products-including-cannabidiol-cbd
https://www.fda.gov/food/hfp-constituent-updates/fda-responds-three-gras-notices-hemp-seed-derived-ingredients-use-human-food
Florida hemp statute §581.217 contains hemp extract definitions, contaminant testing/label requirements and restrictions including age 21+ for ingestion/inhalation hemp extract products. Application to actual dried infusible flower requires expert legal classification; state permissibility does not override federal food law.
https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/581.217
Check anticipated federal definition changes effective November 2026 before procurement/marketing. Do not assume today's cannabinoid flower product will remain lawful for planned distribution.

## Food safety / scientific research
- Source identity: botanical species/cultivar, part used, harvest conditions, traceability and microbial control for any dried infusible flower.
- Cannabinoids: independently measured CBD/CBDA, THC/THCA and other cannabinoids on flower AND prepared infusion; realistic serving exposure and effects of temperature, preparation and co-ingredients. Infusion extraction can vary significantly; do not imply a predictable CBD dose without validated testing.
- Contaminants: pesticides, metals (especially lead/cadmium), residual solvents if processed, mold/mycotoxins and foreign matter.
- Handling: validated drying, storage, packaging and shelf life; evidence of suitability for human beverage use rather than suitability for smoking or aromatics.
- Potential interactions, adverse effects, driving impairment, pregnancy/breastfeeding warnings; regulatory and clinical assessment before any claims.
- Scientific nutrition benefits and psychoactive/pharmacological effects are different subjects. No claims about pain, sleep, anxiety, euphoria or medical benefits without substantiation and applicable regulatory authorization.

## Early market research
- Compare existing marketed hemp flower teas by ingredient list, labeled cannabinoids, claims, testing documentation, state shipping restrictions, retail channel and pack prices. Competitor presence is NOT evidence of legal compliance or product safety.
- Determine whether viable distribution is state-restricted specialty hemp retail, permitted medical cannabis channel, or cannot be lawfully marketed as proposed.
- Ask a qualified Florida hemp/food lawyer: exact dried-flower classification; brewing-intent food status; labeling/testing; age verification; shipment; effect of 2026 federal amendments.
- Ask food scientists/process authorities whether a valid intended-use safety case can be established for a flower infusion. Do not solicit manufacturing of ingestible cannabinoid product absent review.

## Decision gates
1. Legal memo on exact SKU, intended use, market/jurisdiction, package copy, online shipping and upcoming law changes.
2. Evidence of human ingestion safety for the particular flower + finished prepared infusion, cannabinoid exposure, contaminant controls.
3. Licensed and food-appropriate supply/manufacture and independent QC.
4. Complete unit economics and evidence of consumer demand.
5. Specific owner and separately authorized pilot budget.
