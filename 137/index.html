INTERACTIVE INSTRUMENTS

Explore the numbers

Two small calculators make the numerical relationships tangible. A close numerical value is not, by itself, evidence of a physical connection.

The fine-structure constant

α ≈ 0.00729735
Alpha is dimensionless and describes electromagnetic coupling. The displayed inverse is a rounded low-energy value; precision values depend on the adopted measurement and convention.

The golden angle

θ ≈ 137.507764°
Calculated as 360° / φ², where φ is the golden ratio. It is a geometric angle, not an electromagnetic coupling.

The inverse fine-structure constant and golden angle are numerically close to 137, but they represent different kinds of quantities. No established physics connects them.
