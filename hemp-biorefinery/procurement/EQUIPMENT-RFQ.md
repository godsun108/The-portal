# Vendor/Research Partner Qualification — Hemp Fractionation
Prepared 2026-10-12. **Draft inquiries only; no quote solicitation sent, no purchasing authority.**

## What to ask before specifying a "cross-current reduction machine"
1. Exact process and manufacturer terminology? Cross-current vs countercurrent wash/extraction or size-reduction? Is equipment mechanically separating, chemically fractionating or upgrading sugars?
2. Relevant installed references on agricultural lignocellulosic biomass, particularly hemp hurd; experimental vs commercial readiness.
3. Representative feed moisture, particle-size distribution, fines tolerance, ash/contaminants and tested throughput with independent data.
4. Demonstrated C5 liquor, cellulose solids and lignin recovery, with basis and analytical methods; include yields, grade and losses.
5. Solvent/acid/base types at general engineering level, chemical recovery scheme, equipment metallurgy and pressure/temperature design ratings.
6. Separation, filtration, extraction stages, residue handling, control/automation, instrumentation and cleaning/plugging risks.
7. Utilities, water/effluent/emissions treatment, chemical handling hazards, code compliance and operators required.
8. Pilot/contract research availability, input sample requirements, nonbinding cost estimate and lead time.
9. Commercial warranty, patent/license/IP ownership, performance guarantees, spare parts and installation/commissioning support.
10. Process integration with pulp mills, lignin refiners, sugar purification, bioplastic compounders and third-party fuel refiners.

## Research / technical partner categories
- Decorticator and pulp-processing engineers / manufacturers
- University agricultural lignocellulosic composition labs
- Biomass pretreatment/fractionation pilot facilities
- Xylose and glucose hydrolysate analytical laboratories
- Lignin resin/composite R&D manufacturers
- Licensed industrial fuel catalysis/refining partners
- Industrial process-safety / environmental permit consultants

## No-cost negotiation policy
Research calls and published specifications only unless vendor gives explicit no-fee terms. No samples, paid evaluation, lab test, chemical procurement, process trial, NDA, binding term sheet, environmental permit application or machinery deposit without user approval.
