# Beverage Venture Readiness — 2026-10-10
## Mission
Make three separate NONALCOHOLIC product tracks decision-ready before capital is available: (A) dry cocoa-shell infusion, (B) sparkling tea soda substitute, (C) zero-proof social beverage. Poolside alcoholic RTD stays in its separate repo. No products are authorized for sale, paid production, or testing.

## Work phases (evidence before spend)
**Now, $0 commitments:** document customers and consumption occasions, candidate formulas, competing products, category price points and channels, ingredient lists, claims to avoid, preliminary compliance classification, supplier RFQ questions, 3+ manufacturing leads for each appropriate format, budget line items, and a pilot experiment protocol. Internal formula tasting only with ingredients already confirmed suitable for food use and properly handled; no commercial sampling until compliant.
**Pre-authorization:** obtain written input from qualified ingredient vendors, commercial formulators/process authorities and manufacturers. Model 3 scenarios: bench, limited paid pilot, initial commercial run. Price finished goods delivered including labels, lab work, inventory, selling costs and working capital.
**Once a budget is authorized:** complete IP/brand clearance, legal entity & insurance decision, paid formulation/lab work, product stability and microbial/process validation as applicable, label review, pilot run and controlled sales. Never skip safety and regulatory gates to go faster.

## Launch readiness trigger (ALL required)
- Responsible decision owner, signed-off budget ceiling and funding source.
- Written product brief with formula target, serving/package size, sugar/caffeine policy, channel, positioning.
- Preliminary brand clearance and appropriate legal operating entity/vendor contracting path.
- Every ingredient's intended food use and supplier specifications reviewed; unusual botanicals separately assessed for safety and legality.
- Written quote/contract review for development, testing, manufacturer, minimums and rights to formula/IP.
- Applicable FDA/state food manufacturing, labeling and processing path mapped by qualified reviewer; shelf-stable cans require special attention to microbiology, pH, sealing and process classification.
- Three-case economics (optimistic/base/downside), break-even threshold and cash at risk.
- Named test channel, predeclared customer-feedback metrics and stop condition.

## Priority and speed
1. **Sparkling Tea Soda**: write 3 flavor briefs and benchmark 5 competing products; conventional food ingredients only at first; zero/nonzero sugar variants; caffeine disclosure strategy. Most aligned with mass repeat consumption.
2. **Cocoa-shell Dry Tea**: safety and food-use Gate 0 before a sellable sample, qualify 2 suppliers with COA and traceability; price small dry pack.
3. **Zero-proof Social**: flavor-led mocktail replacement first; avoid promises of alcohol-like euphoria. Functional actives deferred pending legal/safety review.
Each track has an independent STOP/GO; completing one does not clear another.

## Weekly upkeep (15–30 minutes)
- Check supplier replies and document dates.
- Update quote matrix, source evidence and blockers.
- Select a single next low/no-cost action per track.
- Recalculate ready-to-act checklist and do not let vendor quotes expire silently.

## Evidence discipline
Use CONFIRMED (primary record), VENDOR CLAIM, ASSUMPTION, PROPOSAL, or OPEN. No invented costs, medical claims, competitor market share, or regulatory clearances.
Sources for safety scoping: FDA acidified/low-acid canned foods guidance https://www.fda.gov/food/guidance-documents-regulatory-information-topic-food-and-dietary-supplements/acidified-low-acid-canned-foods-guidance-documents-regulatory-information and current state food rules; exact applicability must be assessed for each finished formula and process.
