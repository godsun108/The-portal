# SIGNAL//WAR

A compact turn-based territory tactics cabinet on a 6x6 field. Each side has three units and contests three scoring nodes. The traveler can move one unit to an adjacent empty cell, hold, or pulse an adjacent enemy away. Controlled nodes generate signal; first to eight wins, with a 16-turn fallback.

A win grants `signal-banner`. Win by turn 12 while holding the enemy to two signal or less for mastery artifact `field-crown`. A loss records `lost-ground`.
