# Feasibility, Mass Balance, and Economic Evidence Plan
2026-10-12. Input fields unpopulated; **no invented plant capacities, yields, $/ton or fuel costs**.

## Engineering basis sheet (fill only with measured or quoted evidence)
- Feedstock: hemp species/cultivar, site and legal cultivation status, stalk vs bast vs hurd mass, moisture wet/dry basis, available harvested dry tonnes/year, delivered $/dry tonne.
- Composition: dry wt% ash, cellulose/glucan, hemicellulose/xylan, lignin acid-insoluble/soluble, extractives, proteins, metals, grit. Specify analytical method and lab.
- Physical separation: decortication throughput, bast fraction wt%, hurd fraction wt%, fines, fiber length/damage, actual machinery quote.
- Process extraction: single vs multistage cross-current vs countercurrent, solvent/chemical family and recycle, pulp fines/liquor losses, hemicellulose solubilization, C5 sugar hydrolysis yield, xylose purity and degradation, cellulose retention and enzymatic glucose yield, lignin isolated solids and purity.
- Material accounting: measured feed dry mass = saleable solid streams + solubilized compounds + chemical conversion gases + residues/waste, reconciled on consistent moisture/molecular bases. Track added process reagents and makeup water separately.
- Utilities and safety: process heat, electrical kWh/ton, water withdrawal/recycle/effluent, steam balance, emissions, wastewater contaminants/COD, dust and solvents.
- Products: independently tested specs, customer sample/offtake evidence, prices at actual purity and pack scale (not consumer retail prices for bulk intermediates).
- Capital: equipment, infrastructure, installation, design, permitting, QA lab, freight, controls, operators, maintenance and insurance with contingency.
- Performance: throughput, uptime, product yields, recovery efficiency, cash operating costs and sensitivity scenarios.

## Sample-material accounting boundary
The literature hemp hurd composition (45.66% cellulose, 24.57% hemicellulose, 21.67% lignin) is a useful **benchmark for one measured sample**, leaving 8.10% other constituents on its reported composition basis. This is **not** a prediction of process yield, and cannot simply be multiplied by hypothetical tonnage to claim tonnes of pure cellulose, xylose and lignin.
Hemicellulose xylan-to-xylose mass changes during hydrolysis due to added water, while depolymerization losses/inhibitor formation reduce recoverable xylose. Use laboratory polymer/compositional methodology and reaction stoichiometry in future rigorous accounting.

## Experimental progression
0. Literature scan and commercial customer specification calls.
1. Independently test representative biomass composition/quality.
2. Contract qualified academic or process lab for comparative fractionation tests under an approved safety protocol, not unreviewed DIY chemical operation.
3. Verify xylose-rich liquor composition/purity, pulp strength/fiber properties, lignin quality, and mass recovery.
4. Compare outputs under alternative product-allocation splits and clear disposal/water/emissions loads.
5. Request manufacturer/lab feasibility quotations; run vetted process simulation and an independent techno-economic / lifecycle assessment.
6. Choose one commercial core and defer energy-intensive fuel modules until cost/scale are justified.

## Capital and unit-economics model
For each scenario:
Annual sales = SUM(product tonnes/year × realized net revenue/tonne)
Annual operating cost = biomass + chemical/enzyme + utilities + wastewater + maintenance + labor + logistics + QA + waste + overhead.
Contribution and operating margin must be reconciled against capex depreciation, debt service, taxes and working capital to estimate actual cash economics.
Unknown product grade/sales channels = unknown revenue. No additive double-counting of bast/hurd/cellulose/sugars/lignin.

## Product quality gates
- Paper: fiber length and pulp properties, tear/tensile/brightness/impurities and customer mill specs.
- Xylose: industrial vs food grade classification, assay purity, furfural/HMF/acid/catalyst remnants, metals, microbial limits and packaging.
- Bioplastic: distinguish cellulose-fiber filler vs chemically derived polymer vs fermentation-made PHA/PLA precursor; verify compounding and end-of-life claims.
- Fuels: accredited testing for finished hydrocarbons, applicable ASTM/other specifications, distribution regulation and blending approvals; gasoline/diesel are not products of the fractionation machine alone.
