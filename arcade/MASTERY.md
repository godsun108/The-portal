# Arcade mastery circuit

The back cabinet derives power from five real Portal artifacts. No independent unlock flag is authoritative.

- GRAVITY PEARL — DRIFT under Converged physics
- NULL COMPASS — NULL RACER mastery under Converged physics
- SWARM HEART — STAR//SWARM mastery under Converged physics
- PHASE CHORD — PULSE//4 S-grade under Converged physics
- VOID FEATHER — complete UP//VOID under Converged physics

The circuit is complete only while all five artifacts are present in canonical local artifact state.
