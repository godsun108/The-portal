# PORTAL Campaign Mode

Bring back a real authored campaign for mobile while preserving open-world freedom.

**First Flight** is the narrative spine through the Continuous Universe vertical slice:
apartment -> street -> vehicle -> spaceport -> spacecraft -> atmosphere -> orbit.

Mobile principles: short-resume checkpoints, durable progress, touch-friendly mission beats, optional exploration between objectives, and cross-device-ready state. Small sessions should advance a real campaign rather than become disposable mini-games.

The campaign runtime owns story progression and checkpoints; the world runtime owns continuous space; EIDOLON owns actors; narration/voice can provide dialogue, radio and mission control.

Campaign is a mode, not the whole universe. The same world can later support free roam, cooperative play and creator-made stories.
