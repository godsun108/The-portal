Our editorial commitments

Every recension, transcript, translation and recording must have its own provenance. Sanskrit accents, meter, sandhi and oral recitation are not interchangeable with a plain English rendering. We will distinguish historical scholarship from living practices and will never create or label synthetic chanting as an authentic traditional recording.
