# SEB-001-G0-004 — Phase Zero Execution Plan

**Revision:** 0.1 | **Date:** 2026-11-26 | **Status:** Proposed owner work plan; no expenditure authorization

## 1. Objective
Convert the existing conceptual project into an evidence-backed pre-FEED decision package while preserving the approved design basis: continuous multistage cross-current concept to evaluate; graphene system C as separately gated carbon R&D; three core product pathways equal strategic priority; 1 → 5 → 10 → 25 dry t/day modular capacity; separate Florida industrial property near Sacred Earth Center 001; product families developed together subject to qualification and profitability.

## 3. Immediate sequence
1. Freeze revision 0.1 design basis and open-items register.
2. Complete no-cost desk research and supplier/customer discovery.
3. Agree sampling protocol with a qualified biomass laboratory before taking samples.
4. Obtain owner approval before sample collection/shipment or any laboratory cost.
5. Populate stream register only from measured or vendor-verified data.
6. Run route comparison with unknowns shown explicitly; no forced numerical winner.
7. Prepare G1 decision paper and present evidence, risks, budget and options for owner decision.

## 4. G0/G1 decision package
Required contents: source/lot register; lab assay reports; decortication trial report; three product requirement sheets; process selection scorecard; stream register; preliminary mass and utility balance; HSE/environmental review scope; site screen; cost model with evidence status; risk register; change log; recommended next stage and capped budget.

## 5. Constraints and non-authorizations
This plan does not authorize land acquisition, lease or option, grant/loan representations, paid consulting, sample shipment, laboratory testing, vendor trials, NDA, equipment RFQ, deposits, contracts, permits, construction or operations. The user retains approval over commitments. All physical tests must be conducted by qualified facilities with appropriate safety and environmental controls.
