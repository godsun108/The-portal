## Segment ledgers; do not blend incompatible economics
| Segment | Essential input | Revenue evidence | Operating costs | Capex/permits/quality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Food mushrooms | substrate, spawn, water, cycles, contamination rate, harvest kg | contracted $/kg, repeat orders, spoilage discounts | labor, cooling, sanitation, energy, packaging, freight, wasted batches | growing rooms, food safety, inspection, cold chain |
| Mycelium packaging | substrate kg, molds, crop time, press/dry losses, pieces produced | paid pilot or LOI, confirmed customer spec | labor, drying, energy, tooling, shipping by volume, rejects | fire/durability/product testing, production space |
| Interior panels | unit area, density, yield, quality rejects | accredited performance standards and qualified customers | finishing, storage, transport, defect warranty | building/fire product certification |
| Fungal enzymes | substrate consumed, target enzyme activity, purification and stability | paid offtake vs enzyme vendor alternatives | culture growth, separation, concentration, QC, carbohydrates lost | containment, assays, industrial bioprocess validation |
| Spent substrate compost | dry tonnage from counted upstream streams | verified field buyers or on-site agronomic value | hauling, screening, compliance, nutrient tests | land application/soil amendment rules |
| Spent substrate biochar | dry tonnes, carbon %, ash, contaminants, suitable end use | independent of any credit claims | drying, energy, pyrolysis, stack controls, testing | air permits, PAHs/heavy metal certification |
| Machine parts | purchased vs internal bill of materials and reliability | demonstrated cost avoided, not hypothetical "sales" | tooling, rejects, labor, maintenance, inspection | mechanical, fire, electrical and food contact qualification |

## Profit-maximization rule
For 1 dry tonne of clean hemp hurd or eligible mushroom substrate, compare:
NET VALUE(route) = realized sale value of independently qualified outputs minus allocated feedstock, culture/chemical, labor, utility, QA, packaging, logistics, waste, compliance and opportunity costs.
Include time to cash and probability of defective batches. A second cascade only increases total value if its incremental revenue exceeds incremental expenses and forgone alternative uses.
Do NOT simultaneously count mushrooms + full biomass char yield + full unconsumed sugar yield from the same substrate.

## First measurable studies
1. Quantify regional sources of clean hemp hurd and culinary mushroom substrate.
2. Compare oyster and lion's mane local B2B purchase interest and price against actual growing cost. No reported numbers until observed.
3. Qualify a packaging buyer with one geometry/protective performance requirement, MOQ and willingness to test.
4. Evaluate actual fungus/hemp-hurd suitability in a qualified lab: growth, contamination, conversion, product quality and residual mass.
5. Measure waste stream quantities and suitability for compost, biochar or anaerobic digestion; choose one.
6. Engineering consultation for zoning, electrical loads, ventilation/air exchange, humidity, cooling, water, fire safety and sanitary separation.
7. Document quotes and independently reviewed ROI scenarios for **three candidate pilots**: food mushrooms, protective packaging and spent-substrate biochar.

## Kill criteria
No demand, excessive spoilage/contamination, poor composite durability, failed tests, unqualified feedstock, no lawful food handling route, unsupported carbon accounting, or unfavorable comparison with existing suppliers -> defer or kill that module.
