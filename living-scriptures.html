Reading & reconstruction

The Bible reader currently contains the World English Bible source corpus, which does not yet include every biblical writing preserved across traditions. Our goal is one inclusive reading collection, not a menu of denominational editions. Missing books must be sourced, rights-cleared and added as readable text, not represented by links or placeholders. Other traditions and ancient writings are presented here with source links rather than unlicensed reproductions. Passage alignment and additional witnesses remain future work. No synthetic reconstruction will be presented as an ancient manuscript.

Editorial commitments

Canon membership is tradition-specific. Composition dates and authorship are often debated. Manuscript age is not identical to composition age. Noncanonical does not mean suppressed, and older does not automatically mean more authentic.
