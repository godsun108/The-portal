# Track C — Zero-Proof Social Beverages
**Stage:** concept / pre-R&D; no active ingredients or claims selected.
**Customer job:** enjoyable adult-feeling beverage to choose instead of alcohol at gatherings.
**Phase 1 focus:** flavor, ritual, social identity; cocktail-like structure (bitterness, acidity, aroma, tannin, texture) without ethanol. Candidate concepts: citrus spritz, dark-berry bitter botanical, tropical ginger-lime. Do not market as intoxicating.
**Functional ingredients:** distinct higher-risk investigation track only. Assess food-use legality, toxicology, interaction risks, intended population, dosage, labeling and substantiated claims before evaluating inclusion. Kava, blue lotus, kratom, cannabinoids or unapproved additives are NOT preapproved for use in any formula.
**Research queue:** map 5–8 comparable nonalcoholic drinks; occasion-based tasting brief; safe conventional beverage ingredients and COAs; format, co-packer and stability path; whether to share neutral base with Sparkling Tea Soda without compromising flavors.
**Gate to pilot:** regulatory path verified for intended ingredients and claims, consumer validation, label design, fully costed quote and appropriate manufacturer.
