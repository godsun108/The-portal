# SEB-001-HSE-001 — Safety and Environmental Basis

**Revision:** 0.1 | **Date:** 2026-11-26 | **Status:** Preliminary hazard basis; formal studies pending

## 1. Purpose
Identify material process, occupational, environmental and site risks to be addressed in design. This is not a hazard analysis, safety case, operating procedure, permit determination or authorization to build or operate. Qualified process-safety, industrial-hygiene, fire-protection and environmental professionals must complete formal studies.

## 2. Principal hazard register
| Hazard area | Credible concern | Required design response / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Feedstock / fiber / fines | Combustible dust, ignition, entanglement, noise, ergonomic injury | Dust hazard analysis where applicable, housekeeping, isolation, guarding, extraction and exposure assessment |
| Drying / hot solids | Fire, smoldering biomass, hot surfaces | Engineered temperature monitoring, fire detection/suppression, safe shutdown and storage controls |
| Wet fractionation | Chemical exposure, corrosive liquids, incompatible materials, spills | Chemical inventory review, compatibility, containment, closed transfer, eyewash/showers and emergency planning |
| Solvent systems (if selected) | Flammable/toxic vapor, static ignition, VOC emissions | Solvent substitution review, classified-area design, bonding/grounding, ventilation, detection and recovery |
| Pressure / thermal systems | Overpressure, rupture, steam/hot-liquid release | Pressure equipment code review, relief design, inspection and safe isolation |
| Wastewater | pH extremes, organics, salts, toxic contaminants, biological hazards | Segregation, characterization, treatment, monitoring and permitted disposal |
| Pyrolysis / gas | CO, flammable syngas, VOCs, PAHs, oxygen ingress, fire/explosion | Engineered reactor and gas train, gas detection, ventilation, flare/oxidizer decision, interlocks and emissions permit |
| Char handling | Hot char, self-heating, combustible dust | Validated cooling, temperature monitoring, enclosed transfer and fire controls |
| Graphene-like carbon R&D | Fine respirable particles/nanomaterials, high electrical power, off-gas, contamination | Enclosed process, source capture, exposure verification, specialized electrical safety and hazardous waste characterization |
| Composite fabrication | Fiber/carbon dust, polymer fumes, VOCs, machining exposure | Local exhaust, material SDS review, exposure assessment and waste controls |
| Site / climate | Hurricane, flood, lightning, heat, salt corrosion | Site-specific civil/structural criteria, emergency power and storm preparation |
| Transport / storage | Chemical and product compatibility, spills, fire loading | Segregated storage, inventory limits, labeling and transport compliance |

## 3. Required formal studies and management systems
- HAZID during concept design; HAZOP or equivalent on developed P&IDs before detailed design / commissioning.
- Dust hazard analysis and explosion protection review for relevant combustible solids.
- LOPA / SIL assessment only where hazard review indicates it is needed.
- Fire protection and emergency response plan coordinated with local authorities and insurer.
- Chemical process safety / OSHA applicability review, including process safety management applicability screening.
- Industrial hygiene exposure assessment for dust, vapors, acids/alkalis, CO and engineered carbon/nanomaterial aerosols.
- Environmental aspects register: air emissions, wastewater, stormwater, hazardous/nonhazardous waste, noise, traffic, water use, soil/groundwater and lifecycle impacts.
- Management of change, permit-to-work, lockout/tagout, confined-space, contractor control, training, incident reporting and emergency drills.

## 4. Graphene and carbon containment principle
Biochar is not graphene. Carbon upgrading is a separate R&D process. Carbon powder and any graphene-like material must be contained, captured, labeled and handled under a documented occupational exposure strategy. Do not discharge uncharacterized carbon aerosol or use an open collection arrangement. Verify containment and worker exposure before any scale-up.

## 5. Environmental acceptance gates
- Identify applicable federal, Florida, county and local permits before site commitment.
- Establish air emission inventory and control strategy for dryers, thermal conversion, boilers/oxidizers and process vents.
- Characterize all wastewater and solid residues before reuse, land application or disposal.
- Test soil-grade biochar batches for relevant carbon stability, PAHs, metals, ash, pH and other applicable quality criteria.
- Perform lifecycle and carbon accounting before making regenerative, carbon-negative, zero-waste or emissions claims.
- Separate food/biological operations from industrial chemicals, thermal systems and carbon materials.

## 6. Hold points
No chemical process trials, pyrolysis operation, graphene upgrading, process construction, permit filing or equipment procurement is authorized by this document. Formal risk studies and approvals precede any physical implementation.
