# Cocoa Shell Tea — Family Brief

## Thesis
An ingredient-led cacao-shell infusion venture beginning with carefully qualified dry loose tea, expanding to botanical blends only after validation, and potentially RTD later. Build the customer relationship and an evidence trail before financing inventory.

## Working plan
1. Confirm ingredient is suitable and permissible for intended beverage use.
2. Qualify food-grade suppliers and review lot-specific safety documentation.
3. Develop and blind-test beverage preparation and taste.
4. Determine packaging, labels, local regulatory path and per-bag costs.
5. Authorize a small compliant market experiment only if preceding gates pass.

No formation, trademark clearance, manufacturer contract, sample order or spend is authorized by this brief.
