THE FIVE-MINUTE BRIEF

Start simple. Validate carefully. Scale what people reorder.

Phase 1

Dry cocoa-shell infusion, subject to food-safety and legal-use clearance. Test aroma, taste, serving yield, packaging and willingness to repurchase.

Phase 2

Signature botanical blends; each ingredient, label and claim reviewed independently.

Phase 3

Ready-to-drink cocoa beverages, requiring a separate food processing, shelf-life and manufacturing plan.

PROCUREMENT

Qualify the ingredient, not just the vendor.

Ask each supplier for edible-use representation, country/region and harvest origin, processing details, lot traceability, certificate of analysis, microbiology, heavy-metal data (including lead and cadmium), moisture/handling specifications, allergen cross-contact statements, shelf-life guidance, MOQ and delivered pricing.

MEASUREMENT

A per-bag economic model, built from quotes.

Sell price − cacao shells − packaging − labels − batch testing allocation − processing labor − payment fees − freight/fulfillment − spoilage − channel fees = contribution per bag.
