01 · Prepare your session

Use comfortable stereo headphones at a low volume. Choose a gentle beat difference and a short session. The tones are synthesized on this device; no audio is uploaded.

Ready when you are. Audio begins only when you press Start.

02 · Guided attention

Arrive. Settle into a supported position. Let your shoulders soften. Notice the contact between your body and the surface beneath you.

Breath. Allow breathing to find a comfortable rhythm. There is no need to force a pattern. When attention wanders, gently return to the next breath.

Notice. Observe sounds, sensations, images or thoughts without needing to interpret them. If anything feels uncomfortable, stop the audio, open your eyes and orient to the room.

Return. Before getting up, notice the room, move your hands and feet, and take your time returning to ordinary activity.

03 · Session journal

Your notes stay in this browser unless you export them. Record observations, not conclusions: what you noticed, when it happened, and how certain you feel.

Safety & evidence. This is a wellness/curiosity experiment, not medical treatment or a diagnostic tool. Binaural beats are an auditory illusion created by presenting slightly different tones to each ear; evidence for reliable brainwave entrainment or specific cognitive effects is mixed and limited. Never use while driving, cycling, operating equipment or anywhere you need full awareness. Stop if you feel dizzy, anxious, uncomfortable or unwell. If you have a seizure disorder, significant sound sensitivity, or a condition affected by sensory stimulation, ask a qualified clinician before experimenting. Keep volume low and sessions optional.
