# EarthCoin (EC) genesis and first-purchase specification — DRAFT

Status: no EC issued, no network mainnet, no purchase authorization.

## Recommended separation
EC is the proposed native network token for fees and protocol participation. EC grants **no automatic rights** to revenue from PORTAL, creators, marketplace services, treasury reserves, or other EarthChain assets; those would require separate lawful agreements and instruments. No fixed USD peg, redemption promise, profit guarantee, or appreciation claim.

## Decisions requiring owner and legal review
- Protocol consensus and independent validators; chain ID, genesis hash, node software, security model and transaction finality.
- Supply model: fixed or inflationary; exact initial and maximum supply; genesis allocations; vesting and lockups; mint/burn authority.
- Wallet: user self-custody vs regulated custody, hardware/mobile signing, recovery and phishing protections.
- Issuer: legally established entity, governance powers, beneficial ownership and conflict controls.
- Rights: governance, fee utility, consumer disclosures, token-holder limitations.
- Sale: permitted jurisdictions, compliance classification, disclosures, purchaser eligibility and tax reporting.
- Payment: specific written authorization for EC sale from each provider; do not assume Apple Pay/Google Pay/PayPal or Stripe automatically support this token.
- Secondary trading: no guaranteed listing, liquidity, transferability or resale.

## First genuine purchase acceptance criteria
1. Independent network validators verify a real EC transfer on the actual deployed network.
2. Production wallet verifies destination address and transaction finality.
3. Counsel and authorized provider confirm the proposed offering, sale, custody and payments are permitted.
4. Executable quote discloses amount, fees, spread, expiry, refunds and restrictions.
5. Provider-confirmed settlement precedes final accounting credit; chain transfer is independently verified.
6. Reconciliation verifies customer obligations and safeguarding assets; discrepancies halt processing.
7. Customer receives receipt, transaction ID and clear dispute/support process.
8. Written explicit authorization before enabling payments, deployment or issuance.

## Immediate zero-cost milestone
Build a local, valueless, multi-validator testnet simulator and test genesis allocations, conservation, double-spend resistance, replay rejection, invalid signatures, validator disagreements, chain restarts and deterministic state transitions. Do not call this a production blockchain or live token.

## Open decisions
EC token supply and economic policy are not chosen. Network implementation and legal issuer remain unapproved. No user payment can be accepted.
