# GLYPH//LOCK

A nine-lock symbolic deduction cabinet. At the beginning of each run, five glyphs are secretly assigned five mathematical operators. The cabinet never states those definitions; it provides input/glyph/output examples and asks the traveler to infer a new output.

Rounds 1-3 use one glyph, 4-6 compose two, and 7-9 compose three left-to-right. Six correct answers grants `glyph-fragment`; a perfect 9/9 grants `grammar-key`. Fewer than six records `syntax-burn`.
