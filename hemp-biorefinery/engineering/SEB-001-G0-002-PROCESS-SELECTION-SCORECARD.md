# SEB-001-G0-002 — Process Selection Scorecard

**Revision:** 0.1 | **Date:** 2026-11-26 | **Status:** Evaluation framework; no route selected beyond owner concept

## 1. Purpose
Compare process routes on a common hemp-hurd feed basis and common product requirements. The owner's cross-current selection remains the concept to evaluate, not proof that a particular commercial machine or chemistry has been selected. The phrase “cross-current reduction machine” must remain split into (i) mechanical size reduction and (ii) staged extraction/fractionation until clarified by process definition and vendor evidence.

## 2. Candidate route families
| Candidate | Description | Principal questions |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Multistage cross-current extraction / washing | Stage efficiency, solvent/reagent consumption, liquor dilution, solids transfer and wash recovery |
| R2 | Countercurrent multistage extraction | Concentration driving force, solids transport, plugging, control complexity and recycle |
| R3 | Hydrothermal / water-based pretreatment | Hemicellulose solubilization, severity, inhibitors, water/heat demand and lignin alteration |
| R4 | Dilute-acid pretreatment / hydrolysis | C5 recovery, corrosion, neutralization salts, inhibitor formation and wastewater |
| R5 | Alkaline fractionation / pulping | Delignification, cellulose retention, chemical recovery, salts and lignin quality |
| R6 | Organosolv fractionation | Lignin quality and solvent recovery, flammability/toxicity, corrosion and capital burden |
| R7 | Enzymatic hydrolysis as a downstream step | Enzyme cost, residence time, solids loading, sugar yield and feed pretreatment dependency |

These are not mutually exclusive full-plant alternatives. A viable process may combine pretreatment, staged washing, solid/liquid separation, enzymatic conversion and product purification. Compare integrated flowsheets, not isolated reactor names.

## 3. Weighted evaluation criteria
Use a 1–5 score only after evidence is available: 1 = poor / unsupported; 3 = acceptable with material gaps; 5 = strong independently supported result. “Unknown” is not a 3; leave blank and mark evidence gap. Suggested weights are provisional and must be approved at the G1 review.

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence needed |
|---|---:|---|
| Product quality / ability to meet three priority pathways | 20% | Independent assays and customer specifications |
| Component recovery and total mass closure | 15% | Pilot mass balance and analytical methods |
| Water, energy and reagent demand | 15% | Metered pilot/vendor data |
| Safety, emissions and wastewater burden | 15% | HAZID-level review and characterized effluent/off-gas |
| Scale-up and demonstrated operating history | 10% | Reference installations and comparable feed |
| Capital and operating cost evidence | 10% | Comparable vendor budget quotations and cost basis |
| Reliability, maintenance, fouling and cleanability | 10% | Pilot run hours, plugging and maintenance records |
| Modularity and future expansion fit | 5% | Layout, utility and package interfaces |
| **Total** | **100%** | |

## 4. Mandatory disqualifiers / hold conditions
A route cannot be recommended for scale-up if it lacks a credible safety and environmental pathway, cannot reconcile its mass balance, relies on unverified product claims, has no plausible wastewater/off-gas treatment path, or depends on proprietary claims that cannot be independently tested. A low apparent reagent cost cannot offset unacceptable worker exposure or uncontrolled emissions.

## 5. Common comparison basis
All candidates shall use the same characterized hurd feed lot, dry mass basis, particle-size envelope, target product specifications, product allocation rules and accounting boundary. Record:
- Dry feed rate and moisture.
- Cellulose/glucan, hemicellulose/xylan, lignin, ash and extractives entering.
- Reagent/solvent/water inputs and recovered/recycled amounts.
- Cellulose-rich solids, C5 liquor, lignin-rich fraction, fines, residues, gases and wastewater.
- Product purity, moisture, inhibitor/contaminant levels and saleable fraction.
- Heat, electricity, water, labor, consumables and waste treatment.
- Run time, upset events, fouling, cleaning and maintenance.

## 6. Decision rule
Do not select a route by weighted score alone. First apply mandatory safety, legal, mass-closure and product-specification gates. Then rank only evidence-backed alternatives, publish score rationale and uncertainty, and perform sensitivity analysis on weights. The final recommendation requires independent process engineering review and owner approval.

## 7. Required output at G1
A route comparison report, selected block-flow configuration, PFD revisions, stream table, preliminary equipment duty list, water/energy balance, HSE review, preliminary cost range with estimate basis, and list of unresolved scale-up risks.
