# GRAVITY//WELL

A precision orbital-physics cabinet. Drag away from the probe and release to apply one of six limited impulses. Gravity wells continuously bend the trajectory. Collect three signal nodes, then enter the landing ring at sufficiently low speed.

Converged Portal physics modulates well strength. A successful landing grants `orbit-dust`; finishing with at least two impulses remaining grants mastery artifact `gravity-seal`. Losing the probe records `lost-probe`.
