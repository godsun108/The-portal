# Mushroom and Mycelium Product Portfolio — Route Comparison
2026-10-12 — Business hypotheses; no verified gross margins, yields or offtake.

| Stream | Candidate output | Sale/customer hypothesis | Readiness question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gourmet mushrooms | Fresh oyster, lion's mane, shiitake grown on separately qualified food-safe media | Chefs, restaurants, grocers, direct buyers | Delivered shelf life, labor, yield/cycle, repeat purchase, substrate compatibility, demand |
| Processed edible fungi | Dried mushrooms, culinary powder, conventional mushroom soup bases | Food retailers and food-service | Lawful food-processing, microbial controls, full traceability, labeled shelf life |
| Growing materials | Spawn or grow-ready blocks for permitted non-psychoactive culinary species | Hobby growers, small farms | Supplier strain rights, contamination and shipping, returns, buyer CAC |
| Molded mycelium packaging | Protective inserts, trays and branded packaging | Brands replacing expanded polystyrene | Cost vs incumbent, moisture, density, vibration/crush testing, tooling time |
| Nonstructural interiors | Acoustic or decorative panels | Interior designers, furniture/fit-out businesses | Fire, smoke, moisture, mold, strength, emissions and applicable building-product testing |
| Nonstructural furniture/fixtures | Cabinet panels, partition infill, display fixtures | Commercial interiors and our own campus | Durability, load limits, testing, price vs wood/MDF |
| Mycelium-grown materials | Leather-like sheets or foams | Apparel/furniture | IP, specialized equipment/finishing, abrasion and product standards |
| Enzyme R&D | Laccases/peroxidases, cellulases, lignin-modifying activity | Industrial pretreatment researchers | Real selective benefit for lignin removal vs cellulose/xylose losses, purification, stability |
| Biological remediation | Qualified fungal treatments for selected pollutants | Licensed remediation partners | Pollutant degradation evidence, toxic residual disposal, permitting, no food crossover |
| Spent substrate | Certified compost, soil mix, animal-use only with safety and legal assessment | Agronomic end users | Contamination, nutrients, salts, agricultural testing, local transport |
| Spent substrate | Biochar/carbon products | Soil customers or materials labs | Drying energy, PAHs/metals, elemental carbon and stability, price/ton |
| Spent substrate | Biogas and process energy | Internal fuel / utility partner | Moisture, digestibility, energy balance, digestate safety, permitted processing |

## Priority hypothesis
1. Investigate customers and unit economics for local gourmet mushrooms plus protective mycelium packaging in parallel.
2. Examine value from spent mushroom substrate only after counting costs, contamination and actual mass availability.
3. Treat enzymes, remediation, novel sheet materials, nutraceutical extracts and structural products as separate R&D lanes; don't claim yields or profitability without data.

## Strict safety distinction
Commercial culinary cultivation uses properly identified, non-intoxicating edible mushroom species and verified food-grade inputs; it does NOT share equipment with industrial biomass treatment or hazardous contaminants. No psilocybin or uncontrolled medical claims in this plan.
