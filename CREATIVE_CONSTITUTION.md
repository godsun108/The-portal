# THE PORTAL — CREATIVE CONSTITUTION

The Portal is not a directory of projects. It is a living artwork made through the internet.

## Five pillars

### REALITY
Let the visitor encounter something that is actually happening.
Truth boundaries are part of the art. Never fabricate live reality.

### ART
Give people experiences whose value can simply be beauty, feeling, strangeness, expression, or impermanence.
Not every room needs utility or progression.

### PLAY
Curiosity is a valid reason to interact.
Games, toys, puzzles, secrets, experiments, and harmless absurdity belong here.
Do not turn play into engagement farming.

### WORLD
The Portal remembers.
Rooms may alter other rooms. Objects may travel. Time may matter. Consequences may surface somewhere unexpected.
Prefer causality over badges and explicit quest lists.

### LEARNING
Teach by experience whenever possible.
Let the visitor manipulate a system, make a prediction, fail, observe, retry, and discover the underlying idea.
Do not disguise unsupported claims as education.

## Creative law

SEE SOMETHING REAL.
MAKE SOMETHING BEAUTIFUL.
PLAY WITH SOMETHING STRANGE.
LEARN SOMETHING TRUE.
LEAVE THE WORLD DIFFERENT THAN YOU FOUND IT.

## Boundaries

- Browser-local state by default.
- No account required for the core world.
- No analytics required to make the experience work.
- Do not claim a source is live when it cannot be verified as live.
- Do not secretly preserve intentionally ephemeral art.
- Do not duplicate canonical systems owned elsewhere in the Turbo ecosystem.
- Mystery may hide explanation; it must not falsify provenance.
- Curiosity is progression. Avoid points, streaks, manipulative retention loops, and achievement clutter.
- Fonzi is a traveler/collaborator, not an explanation machine.

The Portal should feel less like a website with features and more like a place where the internet itself is the medium.
