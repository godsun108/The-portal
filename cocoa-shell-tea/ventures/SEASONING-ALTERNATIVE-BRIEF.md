# Alternative Hypothesis — CBD-Free Hemp Seed Culinary Seasoning
Distinct from the user's original CBD seasoning idea. No formula and no sales authorization.

## Why this lane exists
FDA currently prohibits adding CBD to foods entering interstate commerce; FDA recognizes specific uses for hulled hemp seed, hemp protein powder and hemp seed oil when meeting relevant specifications and other food rules. This creates a potential ordinary-food concept to investigate, not a workaround that makes CBD seasoning legal.

## Consumer hypothesis
People shopping for savory spice blends may respond to ingredient transparency, taste and appealing culinary pairings. Validate buyer demand before manufacturing.

## Proposed research
- Look up 5 comparable seasoning brands and price/oz, pack size, ingredients, distribution and consumer reviews.
- Map intended consumer uses (vegetables, potatoes, proteins) at the concept level.
- Verify precise food-grade hemp seed ingredient meets applicable specifications and food safety standards; never infer all hemp components are GRAS.
- Obtain nonbinding pricing for dry-blend manufacture, small jars/pouches, labels and freight.
- Distinct name and intellectual-property check before package art.
- Blind sensory research only once all ingredients are established as suitable foods, handled safely, and samples are permitted in the setting used.

## Funding trigger
A legal/food-safety vetted product specification + sensible landed cost/retail price + defined compliant test channel. No sales or promotions before approval.
