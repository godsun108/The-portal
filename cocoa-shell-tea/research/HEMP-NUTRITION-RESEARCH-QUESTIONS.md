# Hemp Nutrition and Whole-Plant Foods — Research Questions
Updated 2026-10-12. No commercial food claim, product or formulation cleared.

## Established ingredient lane
Research hulled hemp seeds, hemp seed protein and hemp seed oil for specific uses within FDA-evaluated GRAS notices; match source material, processing and intended use to recognized specifications. Verify FDA food facility / state requirements, accurate Nutrition Facts, allergens, contamination controls, labeling, and grocery distributor qualification before commercial plans.
FDA seed ingredient resource: https://www.fda.gov/food/hfp-constituent-updates/fda-responds-three-gras-notices-hemp-seed-derived-ingredients-use-human-food

## Whole-plant frontier (research only)
Compare seeds, young leaves, microgreens and flowers by dry-weight protein, fiber, fatty acid and vitamin/mineral concentrations *and realistic serving sizes*. Identify cannabinoid/acid profiles, harvest-stage effects, pesticides, microbial hazards, lead/cadmium, and any clinical exposure risks. Cultivation classification as industrial hemp does NOT by itself establish FDA-recognized safety for edible leaf or flower. Do not assume unheated flower lacks psychoactive exposure concerns; processing and product testing matter.
Questions: Which exact cultivar? Which plant tissue? Which analytical method? Which safe human dietary exposure? Which lawful conventional food use? What state/federal legal route?
FDA CBD food position: https://www.fda.gov/news-events/public-health-focus/fda-regulation-cannabis-and-cannabis-derived-products-including-cannabidiol-cbd

## Commercial option
CBD-free hemp-heart culinary seasoning as the simplest concept to investigate; not a completed safety/food-law clearance.
