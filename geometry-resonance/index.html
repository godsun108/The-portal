THE GOLDEN RATIO

φ = (1 + √5) / 2 ≈ 1.618034

The golden ratio is a proportion, not a frequency or a source of energy. When two frequencies have this ratio, they are mathematically related; whether they sound harmonious or produce a useful effect depends on context.

SHAPE STUDIES

Six geometries to investigate

⬭

Egg / ellipsoid

A curved, non-uniform cavity can support acoustic modes shaped by its dimensions, wall material and contents. Egg shapes do not have one universal resonance.

△

Pyramid

Interior cavities and external structures can reflect sound or interact with electromagnetic waves. Claims of exceptional energy concentration require controlled measurement.

◎

Torus

A ring-shaped geometry appears in fluid flow, magnetic configurations and resonant devices. The actual field depends on the source and boundary conditions.

⬡

Dodecahedron

Its twelve pentagonal faces contain golden-ratio relationships. Symmetry can influence resonant mode patterns, depending on construction.

◯

Sphere

Spherical symmetry gives well-studied acoustic and electromagnetic modes. A useful baseline for comparing other shapes.

〰

Golden spiral

A logarithmic spiral whose radius grows by φ every quarter-turn. It is a geometric construction, not a frequency generator.

WHAT SCIENCE CAN TEST

Geometry changes the boundary conditions

Resonant frequencies depend on shape, size, material properties, what fills the space, and how energy enters the system. For a fair comparison, experiments should control those variables and measure the output rather than infer an effect from appearance.

A strong experiment: compare equal-volume cavities made from the same material, excite them with the same calibrated frequency sweep, then record resonance peaks with a microphone. Repeat trials and publish the raw measurements.

THE RESEARCH MAP

Three layers, kept distinct

Established physics

Acoustic standing waves, cavity resonances, electromagnetic modes and geometry-dependent field distributions.

Mathematical patterns

Golden-ratio proportions, Fibonacci arrangements, symmetry, spirals and fractal growth patterns.

Open questions

Whether particular environments or sound patterns measurably affect attention, relaxation or subjective experience—and under what conditions.

No medical, healing, consciousness-enhancement or energy-amplification claims are made here. Those claims require specific evidence and reproducible tests.
