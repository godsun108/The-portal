Get PORTAL onto your onn. TV

The onn. name covers both Roku TV and Google TV models. Pick the home screen you see:

Choose your TV system to see the correct setup. These instructions do not change the film or its audio output.

Connection check: Turn off any existing audio-only AirPlay session, keep iPhone and TV on the same Wi-Fi, then try Screen Mirroring from iPhone Control Center. Check that a video from Photos can mirror before troubleshooting PORTAL video codecs.

Alternative: On a TV with a usable web browser, open godsun108.github.io/The-portal/tv-cinema.html directly on the TV. Browser and codec support vary; Commons WebM files may not play on every TV.

Screen Mirroring (iPhone)

1. Stop audio-only AirPlay and select iPhone as the audio output. 2. Swipe down from the top-right for Control Center. 3. Tap Screen Mirroring (two overlapping rectangles) and select your TV. 4. Return here and press Play. Keep video inside this page rather than opening the native full-screen video player. Your iPhone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi.

Mirroring copies your iPhone display; it is different from sending a video file directly through AirPlay. Some receivers, DRM, or formats may still prevent picture.

How viewing works

Official-source links are discovery links, not PORTAL-hosted movies. Availability, accessibility, territory and playback capability must be checked at the provider. A link to a film project may contain production information rather than a playable film.

PORTAL will only enable in-page Watch Now for a title after checking its specific media URL, embed policy, rights and real-device playback.

Premium licensing roadmap

Stage 1: Confirm rights, territories, term, devices, captions, artwork and allowed playback modes for every proposed title.

Stage 2: Approach independent creators, film aggregators and distributors for nonexclusive digital rights and pilot screening agreements.

Stage 3: Introduce licensed premium collections only after signed agreements, reliable delivery, DRM requirements, payment compliance and reporting are in place.

Catalog inclusion is not permission to host, embed, rebroadcast or monetize a film. Public-domain status must be verified by title, edition and jurisdiction.
