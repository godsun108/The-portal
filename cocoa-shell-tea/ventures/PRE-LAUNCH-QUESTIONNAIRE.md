# No-Cost Manufacturer / Regulatory Inquiry Checklist
Do not send confidential formulas or proprietary brand IP in a first inquiry. This is for future qualifying conversations.

## Supplier — all physical products
1. Legal supplier name/facility address and relevant licenses/inspections?
2. What is the intended use of the exact ingredient or finished SKU?
3. Lot-specific COA, specification sheet, origin, processing, traceability and recall/complaint policy?
4. Microbiology, pesticide, heavy metal, residual solvent and relevant allergen test coverage?
5. MOQ/sample sizes, pricing basis, lead time, shelf life, shipping conditions and payment terms?
6. Can documents be shared without an NDA or fees for preliminary qualification?

## Food product manufacturer — hemp-seed seasoning only if lawful formulation
1. Dry spice blending/packing capability and food facility credentials?
2. Allergen cross-contact, food safety preventive controls, traceability, packaging and labels?
3. MOQ, development/sample fees, testing requirements and COGS quote assumptions?
4. Can customer own formula, art, trademarks and ingredient supplier specifications?
5. Insurance and recall/responsibility boundaries?

## Hemp/cannabinoid and botanical inhalation concepts — legal/scientific *classification* inquiry only
1. Does the entity have the relevant license/certification for the **specific** class and jurisdiction?
2. Has a qualified inhalation-toxicology professional assessed each proposed ingredient and expected combustion exposure?
3. Which state/federal, packaging, testing, age-verification, marketing and shipping regimes apply?
4. Which SKUs or ingredients are prohibited or unworkable, and why?
5. What independent lab tests and insurance would be required?
**Do not ask for blending/manufacturing or human-use trials until regulatory and safety gate passed.**
