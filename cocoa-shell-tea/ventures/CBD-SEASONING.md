# Venture Concept — CBD Seasoning
Status: RESEARCH ONLY / BLOCKED FOR COMMERCIALIZATION. Updated 2026-10-10.

## Idea
Savory culinary seasoning associated with the hemp/cannabinoid concept. Intended physical product. No formula, label, production or sale approved.

## Critical federal gate
FDA states CBD added to food may not be introduced into interstate commerce and CBD is not approved as a conventional food additive. A culinary seasoning intended to be consumed as food does not become exempt simply because it is called a seasoning. State hemp rules do not erase the federal problem. Regulatory counsel must confirm any lawful path before progressing an ingestible CBD SKU. Source: https://www.fda.gov/news-events/public-health-focus/fda-regulation-cannabis-and-cannabis-derived-products-including-cannabidiol-cbd

## Alternative lawful research lane
Explore a separate **CBD-free hemp seed culinary seasoning** made with food-appropriate hemp seed ingredients and culinary spices, subject to ordinary ingredient, allergen, facility and labeling review. Do not claim this is equivalent to a CBD product. FDA identifies hulled hemp seed, hemp seed protein and hemp seed oil as potentially lawful food ingredients subject to requirements; confirm exact intended uses. No recipe or packaging authorized.
