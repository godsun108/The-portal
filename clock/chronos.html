04 / WHAT THIS MODEL MEANS

Quantum model: a probability model for a two-level system and Ramsey-style phase interrogation.

Timekeeping model: a virtual oscillator whose rate can be adjusted using a separately supplied drift estimate.

Important boundary: the emulated quantum measurement does not physically calibrate the device clock. The software clock cannot outperform the host's actual oscillator. No atomic transition is being measured in this browser.
