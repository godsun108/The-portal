Lesson 1: Why a molecule isn't automatically a medicine

A molecule may interact with a biological target in a computer model or test tube. A medicine must also be sufficiently safe, reach the relevant tissue, work at an appropriate exposure, and demonstrate meaningful benefit in well-designed studies.

Four evidence stages

  1. Mechanism: a plausible biological explanation
  2. Preclinical: measurements in laboratory systems
  3. Clinical: appropriately reviewed studies in people
  4. Regulatory & quality: authorization and consistent manufacturing

For example, melittin can disrupt certain lipid membranes in preclinical studies, but that does not establish it as a safe antiviral treatment.

Knowledge check

A computer model predicts that a peptide binds to a viral protein. What does this establish?

Genesis research method

For every scientific claim, record the source, model or experiment, assumptions, observed outcome, uncertainty, and what remains unproven. Simulated binding alone never counts as proven efficacy.
