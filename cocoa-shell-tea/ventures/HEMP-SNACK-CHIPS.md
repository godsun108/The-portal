# Hemp-Based Snack Chip — Research Brief
Date: 2026-10-12
Stage: concept / commercial feasibility only. No testing, production, product claims, spending or launch authorized.

## North star
A delicious, genuinely crunchy, savory snack chip with meaningful hemp-seed-derived ingredients, affordable enough to compete with familiar chips. Product-first positioning: taste, crunch, recognizable flavors, then verifiable nutrition, not cannabinoids or therapeutic effects.

## Legally plausible ingredient starting point
FDA GRAS notices (December 2018):
- Hulled/dehulled hemp seed / hemp hearts (GRN 765), explicitly including snack foods such as **chips** in intended uses, at stated use levels from 1–40% (must verify exact applicability and specifications).
- Hemp seed protein powder (GRN 771), general food uses with exceptions.
- Hemp seed oil (GRN 778), general food uses with exceptions.
All use of particular suppliers/ingredients must match the scope, specifications and regulatory requirements of actual products; FDA no-questions letters are not approval of our as-yet nonexistent chip. See:

## Three product-platform hypotheses (not manufacturing recipes)
1. **Tortilla-style chip:** corn / another verified food starch + hulled hemp hearts and possibly hemp protein, intended for scooping and dipping. First to assess because consumer format is familiar.
2. **Popped or extruded protein crisp:** compliant food-grade starch/legume base with appropriately qualified hemp seed protein. Texture, expansion, color and aftertaste may require engineering.
3. **Baked savory cracker-chip:** conventional grains or pulses plus hulled hemp seeds; potentially simpler equipment or small-batch feasibility but must validate desired crispness.

Do not assume hemp seed alone behaves like potato/corn starch. Product texture must be tested, and formulas are not yet finalized.

## Initial taste / package hypotheses
- Sea Salt (baseline)
- Smoky Lime (bright, savory)
- Garlic Herb (pairs with potential separate hemp culinary seasoning)
Avoid health treatment, sleep/relaxation, euphoria or CBD/THC messaging.

## Required product decisions
- Tortilla vs popped vs cracker; bag weight/servings; regular snack buyer vs higher-protein niche.
- Desired protein/fiber and caloric benchmarks based on actual tested Nutrition Facts; no unsupported 'high protein', 'keto', 'superfood' claims.
- Allergen and cross-contact management; hemp seed COAs and cannabinoid contamination from harvest; heavy metals, pesticide, microbiology and rancidity/oxidation review.
- Oil/process choice and shelf-life: hemp seed oil contains unsaturated fatty acids; heat stability, oxidative rancidity and packaging oxygen barrier must be assessed. No unsupported benefit claims.
- Manufacturers able to process hemp seed ingredients, minimum run, packaging MOQ, changeover, allergen management and certification.
- Retail channels: natural grocery, convenience, DTC multipack, event merch; shipping weight, crush losses and trade margins.
- True cost: starch base + hemp ingredients + flavors + processing + quality testing + packaging + freight + retailer/distributor margin + promotions + returns and waste.

## First no-spend diligence
1. Record 5 current snack competitors with SKU, bag grams, price per oz, protein/serving, hemp inclusion if any and store/channel, citing observed date.
2. Obtain supplier documentation on exact hulled-seed, hemp-seed protein and food oil specs; compare actual GRAS-notice conditions.
3. Determine US manufacturing routes for tortilla/extruded/cracker formats (license, food safety, private-label MOQ and IP terms).
4. Build a conceptual price waterfall and identify minimum gross margin needed at affordable retail.
5. Decide which base format is easiest **after** feasibility/quote evidence, not intuition.
6. Develop compliant nutrition claims only after nutrition testing/labeling review.

## Gates to capital
Written intended-use legal/food safety review; reliable food-grade hemp supplier and QA documentation; food manufacturer scope; costed prototype budget; explicit cap and loss limit; legally suitable customer sampling plan. No expenditure without user authorization.
