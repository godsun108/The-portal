# SEB-001-UTL-001 — Utilities and Infrastructure

**Revision:** 0.1 | **Date:** 2026-11-26 | **Status:** Preliminary basis / loads TBD

## 1. Scope
Establish the utility and site infrastructure design basis for a separate 5–10-acre Florida industrial property near Sacred Earth Center 001. No parcel, utility provider, service capacity, water source, discharge point or permit has been selected.

## 2. Capacity basis
Initial feed: 1 dry t/day, 16 h/day, 250 days/year; 62.5 kg/h dry feed. Future modules: 5, 10 and 25 dry t/day. Site master planning shall reserve corridors and expansion footprints, but utilities shall be installed in stages and sized from verified equipment loads, not speculative ultimate demand.

## 3. Utility systems and data required
| Utility | Preliminary system concept | Sizing inputs / acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Electrical | Utility service, switchgear, distribution, motor control, separately protected high-power R&D zone | Connected load, demand, starting currents, harmonics, fault study, arc-flash and future expansion |
| Process water | Metered supply, storage and segregated process-water network | Water balance, quality by use, withdrawal limits and drought resilience |
| Cooling | Closed-loop cooling where technically appropriate | Heat rejection, temperatures, water chemistry and redundancy |
| Thermal energy | Heat recovery assessment; independent thermal conversion package | Steam/hot-water/fuel duty, temperature levels, emissions and reliability |
| Compressed air | Instrument and service air separated where needed | Pressure, flow, dew point, oil/particle quality and criticality |
| Nitrogen / inert gas | Only where process hazard review requires | Demand, storage, oxygen-deficiency hazards and supply reliability |
| Wastewater | Segregated process, sanitary and stormwater systems | Flow, pH, COD, salts, organics, metals, solvents and permit limits |
| Fire water | Code- and insurer-reviewed fire protection | Hazard inventory, hydraulic calculation, water source, access and response time |
| Ventilation / dust collection | Local exhaust and enclosed transfer | Dust explosibility, exposure limits, capture velocity and emissions treatment |
| Data / controls | Segmented OT network, historian, alarms and secure remote monitoring | Availability, cybersecurity, backup, safe-state behavior and maintainability |

## 4. Site infrastructure
Survey and due diligence must establish parcel boundaries, zoning/land use, flood zone/elevation, geotechnical conditions, wetlands, drainage, stormwater, road/turning access, setbacks, neighboring receptors, overhead/underground utilities, power capacity, water rights/service, wastewater availability, fire response and environmental history. Florida hurricane wind, flood, heat, lightning and salt/corrosion exposure must be included in design criteria by qualified local professionals.

## 5. Water and waste doctrine
Use separate metering for process makeup, cooling, sanitation, fire testing and irrigation. Reuse water only after chemistry and cross-contamination review. No process effluent, ash, char, condensate or nutrient stream is presumed suitable for discharge or land application. Maintain sampling points and chain of custody.

## 6. Acceptance criteria
- Site utility availability letters or verified provider data.
- Preliminary electrical load list and one-line concept.
- Water balance and wastewater characterization.
- Fire-water and access concept reviewed by fire protection professional / authority having jurisdiction.
- Stormwater and flood basis supported by parcel survey.
- Utility corridor plan reserves future modules without overbuilding initial services.
- Metering strategy supports mass, water, energy and environmental accounting.
