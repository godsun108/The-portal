# Eight Industry Markets — Engineering Qualification Matrix
2026-10-12. Concept research only. This list is not exhaustive and standards vary by jurisdiction, part and customer.

| Sector | Potential early parts | Long-term ambition | Key qualification domains | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Skyscrapers / construction | Acoustic tiles, nonstructural cladding prototypes, protective packaging, formwork | Engineered façades, load-rated panels, hybrid structural systems | Applicable building code/ICC, ASTM/EN mechanical, fire spread/smoke, moisture, creep, durability, engineered anchorage, professional signoff | No certified components |
| Boats / ships | Interior panels, cabinets, trim, packaging | Hull/deck composites and marine structures | Water absorption, salt/fatigue, flammability, marine class (e.g. ABS/DNV), corrosion/adhesion, long-term durability | No marine qualification |
| Automobiles / trucks | Noncritical interior trim, mats, acoustic liners | Chassis/structural or crash-related components | FMVSS/OEM tests, fire, impact, thermal cycling, VOCs, fatigue, process QC | No auto qualification |
| Trains / rail | Interior trim, acoustic panels | Exterior/structural rail materials | Applicable rail fire/smoke standards, vibration, wear, structural fatigue, operator procurement | No rail qualification |
| Airplanes | Ground fixtures, protective packing, selected noncritical interior prototypes | Certified airframe/interior systems | FAA composite qualification, flammability, smoke/toxicity, impact/damage tolerance, bonded joints, production traceability | No aircraft qualification |
| Spaceships | Ground support tooling, protective covers | Flight-qualified composites, structural panels, thermal/electrical components | NASA-STD-6016/6001, ASTM E595 outgassing where applicable, thermal vacuum/cycling, radiation, ignition, structural strength, mission acceptance | No space qualification |
| Computers / electronics | Casing prototypes, cable management, packaging | Thermally/electrically engineered carbon/fiber composites | Flame ratings, ESD, EMI, electrical insulation vs conductivity, dimensional/thermal stability, device safety | No device qualification |
| Robots / machine tools | Covers, jigs, housings, molds | Qualified high-strength robotic components and internally fabricated industrial tooling | Fatigue/creep, impacts, chemical resistance, fire, repeatability, machine guarding and equipment safety | No certified components |

## Application-specific reality
Material approval is **per finished formulation, manufacturing process, part, facility and intended environment**; coupon success is not building code acceptance, FAA approval or NASA flight qualification. Structural substitution requires designing the entire load path, joints, failure modes and protective systems, not merely comparing tensile strength.

## Suggested entry-sequence hypotheses
A. Packaging / acoustic and non-load-bearing panels.
B. Hemp fiber-reinforced plastics for housings and automotive/marine/robotic noncritical components.
C. Biochar/graphene-like conductive composite R&D for electronics and specialty industrial applications.
D. Code-tested building envelope/interiors and regulated transport materials.
E. Aerospace flight hardware and structural skyscraper systems as multi-year R&D programs.
No profitability ranking is asserted: require validated buyers, volumes, costs and ability to meet standards.
