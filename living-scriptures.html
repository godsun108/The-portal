About the texts

The staged Bible corpus is the public-domain World English Bible (WEB), sourced from eBible.org. It includes 81 books in this edition; that count is edition-specific and does not represent every community's canon. The source data is structurally validated, while the readable projection of original USFM formatting still requires editorial review. 1 Enoch is presented with its tradition and translation identified. A text's inclusion here is not a claim that it was universally canonical, suppressed, or proven to be the oldest form of a work.
