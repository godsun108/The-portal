## Supplier request: documentary due diligence
1. Exact roasted shells/husks with SKU, producer, origin, lot traceability and recent actual ship-ready quantities.
2. Written suitability for packaged **dry infusion beverage ingredient / resale** (distinct from vague culinary wording).
3. Current lot COA for aerobic bacterial load, yeast/mold, pathogens incl Salmonella/E. coli, lead/cadmium/arsenic/mercury, moisture/water activity, mycotoxins (aflatoxins/ochratoxin A where appropriate), pesticide residues and validated test methodologies/detection limits.
4. Processing controls (roasting parameters documented by supplier without asking for trade secrets, hazard analysis, kill step effectiveness, sanitary handling) and allergen/cross-contact information.
5. Pricing by 2.5lb/5lb/25lb/50lb, MOQ, first available date, sample availability/cost, freight to Florida, lead time, documents that can be provided before money is paid.
6. Licensing/manufacturing quality assurance, shelf-life, packaging conditions, ownership/repacking/IP restrictions, return/recall procedure.
7. Test water infusion for metal/caffeine/theobromine exposure before any claims; chemical analysis of dry shells does NOT by itself establish brewed concentration.
