# Zero-Proof Social — First Concepts
Updated 2026-10-12. Research concepts, no finished formulation or claims.

## Customer
Adults who want a deliberate, nonalcoholic drink at social gatherings without a product that mimics intoxication. Occasion: backyard party, restaurant, late-night music/event venue, cookout.

### Concept A — Citrus Bitter Spritz
Bright citrus, layered bitterness, aroma, effervescence and appealing garnish ritual. Differentiation test: does the flavor feel like a deliberate adult choice instead of soda?

### Concept B — Berry Botanical Fizz
Tart fruit, restrained sweetness, herbaceous complexity, sparkling finish. Differentiate by clear ingredients and enjoyable repeat drinking.

## Nonnegotiable boundaries
Use only ingredients whose exact intended food use is supported. Botanical history as herbal medicine is not food authorization. No kava, blue lotus, kratom, cannabinoid or mood/euphoria claim is automatically cleared. Sensory study only when food safety and compliance for ingredient choices are established.

## Next free studies
Compare 5–8 existing nonalcoholic social drinks by pack, price per serving, distribution, bitterness/sweetness cues and brand message (date every observation). Seek end-user feedback on occasions before selecting which product to fund. Determine whether cold chain or shelf-stable route is appropriate; no small-batch shelf-stability assumptions.
