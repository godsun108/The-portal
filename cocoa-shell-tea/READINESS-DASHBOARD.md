## Immediate decision sequence
**P0:** build verified supplier and category evidence (no purchases).
**P1:** complete briefs and channel hypotheses for all three nonalcoholic lanes.
**P2:** get no-obligation quotes and regulatory scope notes.
**P3:** choose ONE product for an authorized, narrow pilot, based on readiness not excitement.
**P4:** execute validated test and evaluate reorder signal before scaling.

## Safety flags
No sale, broad consumer sampling or shelf-stable claims without ingredient and process clearance. Every proposed botanical gets an independent ingredient review. Keep alcoholic and nonalcoholic regulatory pathways separate.
