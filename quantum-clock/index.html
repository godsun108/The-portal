What the clock is doing

01 · PREPAREA π/2 pulse creates a superposition of two atomic energy states.
02 · EVOLVEThe state accumulates phase during a controlled free-evolution interval.
03 · INTERROGATEA second π/2 pulse converts phase difference into a measurable population.
04 · CORRECTA feedback loop can steer a local oscillator toward the atomic resonance.
P(e) = ½ [1 + cos(2π · Δf · T)]

This simplified Ramsey model assumes ideal pulses, a two-level system, and no decoherence. The ideal projection-noise-limited frequency scale shown is δf ≈ 1/(2πT√N); it is a model benchmark, not a certified accuracy claim. Real clocks require atomic preparation, laser or microwave control, environmental correction, calibration, and traceability.
