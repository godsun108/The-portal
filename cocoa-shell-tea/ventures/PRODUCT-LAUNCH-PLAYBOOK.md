## Reusable company system
CONCEPT → VALIDATE CUSTOMER JOB → REGULATORY CLASSIFICATION → INGREDIENT / PRODUCT SAFETY → MANUFACTURING PATH → SUPPLIER QUOTES → LANDED UNIT ECONOMICS → IP AND CONTRACT REVIEW → CAPITAL AUTHORIZATION → VALIDATED PILOT → REORDER EVIDENCE → SCALE.

All stages are independently reviewed per SKU, jurisdiction and selling channel. One product's legal clearance does not apply to another. New facts may reopen any gate.

## No-capital deliverables
- One-page opportunity brief (buyer, job, channel, competitor alternatives, product hypothesis, differentiate-by evidence).
- Separate legal/safety evidence log with sources, dates, applicability and unanswered questions.
- Minimum 3 competitor/comparator listings, exact pack sizes and current prices with dates (do not infer market size from competitor count).
- Supplier candidate directory; send NONBINDING requests for publicly shareable test/specification data only after contact details are verified.
- Manufacturing cost inquiry checklist with no commitment.
- Price waterfall: MSRP - retailer/distributor margin - promotion - freight - landed product cost - returns - payment/fulfillment = contribution.
- Two stage budget requests: Phase 0 research (ideally $0) and capped Phase 1 tests after explicit authorization.
- Definitions for go/no-go: legality verified; product safe for intended use; sufficient margin and cash reserve; clear buyer/channel; repeat demand measurements.

## Weekly review protocol
1. Check vendor inbox and returned attachments; record delivery failures.
2. Record new facts with source and date; archive marketing claims as vendor claims.
3. Update one critical blocker per venture.
4. Compare near-term launch options by readiness, cost at risk, repeat-demand evidence and time to compliant pilot.
5. No production, testing on people, store checkout, deposits, purchase orders or supplier contracts without separate authorization.
