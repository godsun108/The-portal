# Fungal Cascading Systems and Industrial Facility Zoning
2026-10-12. Conceptual flow, not a validated sterile/food manufacturing process.

## Material priority is conditional
Hemp hurd can be sold directly as bedding/building input, biorefined to sugar, used as mycelium substrate, or pyrolyzed to biochar. Allocate only after comparing price per clean dry tonne, conversion efficiency, market, time, production risk and downstream value.
Use independent food-safe biomass for food mushrooms. Industrial chemically treated hurd, pyrolysis soot, solvent residues, contaminated biosolids, and remediation material must NEVER enter a culinary mushroom substrate flow.

```
HEMP FARM / OTHER QUALIFIED AGRICULTURAL SOURCES
      -> audited hemp decortication -> bast -> paper / fiber-reinforced parts
                                  -> qualified hurd (split by allocation)
                                     A -> edible fungi substrate -> mushrooms -> sale
                                     |                       -> spent block -> screened recovery
                                     B -> composite growth -> molded protective products
                                     |                    -> spent/cuttings -> screened recovery
                                     C -> chemical fractionation -> xylose/cellulose/lignin
                                     D -> clean residue -> pyrolysis -> tested biochar
SPENT SUBSTRATE (each lot allocated once)
      -> fertilizer/compost if approved OR biochar if accepted feed OR biogas if feasible
      -> wastewater, exhaust, dust, pathogen/contaminant controls (measured)
FACTORY TOOLING
      -> own hemp-fiber reinforced panels, mold shells and protective covers
      -> purchased/certified controllers, drive motors, bearings, pressure vessels, wiring
```

## Food-safe zone (separate air, sanitary design, water and staff hygiene)
Incoming food-grade raw materials, substrate preparation, inoculation/containment, fruiting, harvesting, cold storage and compliant packing. Log strain identity and food traceability. Do not assume all mushrooms/substrate are edible or free from allergens/contaminants.

## Composite zone
Qualified biomass blend, molds, colonization, controlled drying/inactivation, mechanical/acoustic/moisture testing, finishing and dust containment. Validate that product is not alive, shedding or growing mold before household sales. Avoid load-bearing claims without independent certification.

## Biorefinery and thermal zone
Engineered decortication, sugar fractionation, chemical recovery, wastewater, biochar pyrolysis and carbon-material upgrading. No shared drains/airflow or unqualified reclaimed heat with clean food work. Heat exchange can be indirect and engineered.

## Remediation zone
Completely separate experimental fungal pollutant processing. Polluted material remains controlled waste until treatment and analysis demonstrate appropriate lawful disposition; never cycle that fungi or substrate into food or soil on assumption.

## Resource integration
- Recover waste heat only through inspected utility loops and hygienic isolation.
- Recover process water only after verifying contaminant/sanitation suitability for destination use; do not return chemical-laden water to food production.
- Treat CO2 and humidity/ventilation as safety and energy loads; do not claim crop CO2 fertilization without design.
- Meter input dry matter, each product, losses, off-gases, water and power.
