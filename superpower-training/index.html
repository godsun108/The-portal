Research laboratory

Protocols are proposals until actually conducted. No result is assumed in advance.

UNVERIFIED HYPOTHESIS

Telekinesis / mind–matter

Question: does intention correlate with target displacement beyond environmental movement?

  1. Use a stable, enclosed target and fixed camera.
  2. Record baseline drift and environmental conditions.
  3. Randomize intention and control blocks.
  4. Blind the analyst to trial condition.
  5. Compare displacement using a preselected method; repeat independently.
A paper wheel or psi wheel is highly vulnerable to breath, convection and static. It is not adequate evidence by itself.
UNVERIFIED HYPOTHESIS

Pyrokinesis / thermokinesis

Question: does intention correlate with temperature readings under controlled conditions?

  1. Begin with a stable object and non-contact temperature sensor.
  2. Log room temperature, airflow, distance and sensor placement.
  3. Randomize observation and control periods.
  4. Repeat measurements and check instrument drift.
  5. Seek independent replication before interpretation.
No exposed flames, ignition attempts, electrical shocks, skin heating or dangerous experiments. Start with ordinary heat-transfer demonstrations and simulations.
BLINDED PERCEPTION

Remote-viewing-style trial

Question: can a description match a concealed target above chance?

  1. An independent person randomly selects a target.
  2. Participant records impressions before seeing options.
  3. Use decoys and blinded scoring.
  4. Predefine scoring rules and trial count.
  5. Report misses as well as hits.
Do not allow feedback, hints, target substitution or post-hoc changes to scoring.
ESTABLISHED TRAINING FIELD

Physiological self-regulation

Question: can comfortable paced breathing and feedback improve awareness or regulation?

  1. Measure resting heart rate under consistent conditions.
  2. Practice gentle, comfortable slow breathing.
  3. Use HRV feedback if an appropriate sensor is available.
  4. Record comfort, stress and readings.
  5. Compare trends across multiple weeks.
Stop if dizzy, faint or uncomfortable. No hyperventilation or prolonged breath-holding.
ACTIVE TECHNOLOGY

Thought-to-device control

Question: how accurately can intentional signals operate an external system?

  1. Start with accessible input or eye tracking.
  2. Measure task accuracy and response time.
  3. Study non-invasive EEG interfaces.
  4. Compare performance across repeated sessions.
  5. Keep assistive technology distinct from paranormal claims.
CONSCIOUSNESS RESEARCH

Dream and altered-state journal

Question: how do sleep, recall and subjective experiences change over time?

  1. Keep a notebook or voice memo beside the bed.
  2. Record fragments immediately after waking.
  3. Note sleep duration and disruptions.
  4. Separate direct recall from later interpretation.
  5. Review patterns weekly.
Protect sleep. Do not use sleep deprivation or unsafe substances to induce unusual states.

90-DAY FOUNDATION

Development roadmap

Progress means completing careful training and producing interpretable records—not unlocking supernatural powers.

DAYS 1–14

Establish baseline

Learn the rotation, record initial measures, establish safe routines and test the journal.

DAYS 15–45

Build consistency

Repeat skills, improve measurement reliability, refine protocols and document changes.

DAYS 46–75

Controlled investigation

Run blinded experiments, use controls, preregister hypotheses and seek independent review.

DAYS 76–90

Analyze & replicate

Compare baselines, inspect failures and confounds, repeat promising trials.

DELIVERABLE

Research report

Training trends, raw data, protocols, limitations, results and next-cycle recommendations.

OPEN QUESTIONS

Continue inquiry

Keep unexplained results open, but do not promote them to discoveries without replication.
