# Research Source Register — 2026-10-12
These references concern hemp feedstocks and related lignocellulosic platforms; results on corn stover and poplar must not be represented as demonstrated on industrial hemp.

1. Hemp hurd composition and pretreatment (one sample: 45.66% cellulose, 24.57% hemicellulose, 21.67% lignin): https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0926669021005823
2. Industrial hemp biorefinery overview (2022): https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2468012522000074
3. Hemp bast/hurd cellulose composition and processing discussion: https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/14/22/15337
4. Hemp hurd paper/MCC pilot research (2024): https://bioresources.cnr.ncsu.edu/resources/industrial-hemp-hurd-processing-for-microcrystalline-cellulose-production-and-its-usage-as-a-filler-in-paper/
5. 2025 hemp composite review: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42114-025-01314-0
6. 2024 lignocellulosic bioplastic materials review: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214993724002574
7. NREL hydrolysis-to-fuels process design (2015, largely corn stover): https://research-hub.nlr.gov/en/publications/process-design-and-economics-for-the-conversion-of-lignocellulosi-3/
8. NREL co-product and CELF research (2024, modeled processes): https://www.nrel.gov/news/program/2024/biorefinery-model-points-to-a-better-method-at-producing-co-products.html
9. PNNL catalytic upgrading of biomass sugars (2013): https://www.pnnl.gov/publications/catalytic-upgrading-sugars-hydrocarbons-technology-pathway
10. Industrial hemp product-manufacturing systematic review (2024): https://www.mdpi.com/2624-7402/6/3/167

**Evidence gaps**: hemp-specific head-to-head data on extraction configurations, production-grade xylose cost, qualifying lignin resin buyers, lifecycle greenhouse emissions, actual capital scale, licensed hydrocarbon finishing and credible local feedstock supply. All must be evaluated before a plant is claimed viable.
