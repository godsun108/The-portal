How viewing works

Official-source links are discovery links, not PORTAL-hosted movies. Availability, accessibility, territory and playback capability must be checked at the provider. A link to a film project may contain production information rather than a playable film.

PORTAL will only enable in-page Watch Now for a title after checking its specific media URL, embed policy, rights and real-device playback.

Premium licensing roadmap

Stage 1: Confirm rights, territories, term, devices, captions, artwork and allowed playback modes for every proposed title.

Stage 2: Approach independent creators, film aggregators and distributors for nonexclusive digital rights and pilot screening agreements.

Stage 3: Introduce licensed premium collections only after signed agreements, reliable delivery, DRM requirements, payment compliance and reporting are in place.

Catalog inclusion is not permission to host, embed, rebroadcast or monetize a film. Public-domain status must be verified by title, edition and jurisdiction.
