04 / COMPARATIVE HISTORY

One shape, many histories

South Asia · Lakshmi

Lakshmi is associated with auspiciousness, flourishing, fertility, beauty and prosperity. Ashtalakshmi traditions articulate eight forms of abundance. Do not assume every eight-pointed star is specifically her emblem.

Ancient Mesopotamia · Ishtar

An eight-rayed star is associated with Ishtar and the planet Venus in Mesopotamian visual culture. This is a documented cultural symbol, distinct from Lakshmi's traditions.

Islamic geometric art

Eightfold stars and interlaced polygons occur widely in architectural and decorative geometry. Their roles are often mathematical and ornamental, and vary by place and period.

Across later traditions

Eight-pointed forms also occur in Christian, Byzantine, Persian and other decorative settings. Similar geometry does not by itself establish a common religious source.
