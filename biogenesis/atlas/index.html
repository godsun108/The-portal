The atlas organizes general research concepts. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend treatment, or establish cures. Disease categories and evidence summaries below are illustrative, not exhaustive clinical reviews.

Evidence standard

For each research topic, Genesis should eventually connect a defined population, clinically meaningful outcome, comparator, research quality, safety information, and source provenance. A mechanism or simulation is not proof of a cure or enhancement.

Current capabilities: topic browsing and links to external literature searches. Not yet implemented: live literature ingestion, structured citations, model predictions, clinical validation, or a complete disease taxonomy.
