# Stage-gate Roadmap
- **G0 Ingredient eligibility and safety** — establish lawful intended use, applicable testing and appropriate expert/regulatory review.
- **G1 Supplier qualification** — written food-grade use specification, traceability, contaminant/microbiology results, MOQ, samples and pricing.
- **G2 Product testing** — brewing specification, blinded sensory trials, stability and storage hypotheses.
- **G3 Label & economics** — approve accurate ingredient and labeling facts; quote packaging, testing, handling and freight; check contribution margin.
- **G4 Controlled market test** — only with compliant product and sales channel.
- **G5 Demand proof** — measure reorder and unit economics before scaling.
- **G6 RTD exploration** — separate process authority, food manufacturing and shelf-life gates.
