# BioGenesis Grand Mission — Human Restoration and Optimization

## North star
Advance the scientific understanding, prevention, treatment, and eventual cure of human disease while improving healthy human function and well-being. Universal cures and maximal enhancement are aspirational research goals, not current capabilities or promised outcomes.

## Two missions
### Human Restoration
Catalog diseases and conditions using recognized taxonomies; map affected populations, mechanisms, established standards of care, gaps in evidence, and validated outcomes. Prioritize burden, severity, tractability, and equity. Distinguish prevention, symptom management, disease modification, remission, and cure.

### Human Optimization
Investigate strength, endurance, movement, cognition, sensory health, sleep, immune resilience, recovery, reproductive health, mental well-being, and healthy lifespan. Compare lifestyle, rehabilitation, assistive technology, and approved medical approaches before experimental interventions. Recognize tradeoffs and individual variation.

## Scientific operating system
1. Disease and human-function knowledge graph: concepts, sources, mechanisms, interventions, outcomes, contraindications.
2. Evidence engine: systematic-review practices, provenance, publication dates, evidence tiers, conflicts of interest, replication.
3. Visual observatory: verified molecular structures, physiological pathways, organ-system visualizations; distinguish schematic, predicted, and observed.
4. Computational laboratory: reproducible benchmarks and established scientific software, not invented quantum claims.
5. Academy: beginner-to-advanced science education, interactive quizzes, uncertainty literacy.
6. Genesis development journal: append-only research hypotheses, tests, failures, and independently evaluated results.
7. Translational pathway: qualified partners, ethics oversight, safety testing, regulatory compliance, and clinical validation.

## Evaluation rubric for every claimed advance
- Which condition or function, and which population?
- What is the comparator and measured endpoint?
- What is the magnitude, duration, and reproducibility of benefit?
- What are adverse effects and unknowns?
- What level of evidence supports the claim?
- Is it a hypothesis, simulation, preclinical finding, clinical effect, or established treatment?
- Does it improve access, affordability, and patient outcomes?

## Guardrails
No autonomous manufacture or dispensing of unapproved medicines; no human experimentation without proper authorization; no self-experimentation protocols, clinical dosing, or hazardous biological optimization. Human oversight is required for decisions affecting patient safety. No claim of universal cure, enhancement, or AI self-improvement without evidence.

## Roadmap
Phase 0: educational observatory and research charter (in progress).
Phase 1: disease taxonomy and sourced evidence record viewer.
Phase 2: human-function atlas and Academy lessons.
Phase 3: reproducible analysis of published results and validated visualization.
Phase 4: qualified institutional partnerships for translational work.

## Status
