The Gospel of Thomas

A collection of 114 sayings attributed to Jesus, preserved in a complete Coptic copy and earlier Greek fragments. It is not part of most Christian biblical canons. The text and its relationship to the canonical Gospels remain subjects of scholarly discussion.
