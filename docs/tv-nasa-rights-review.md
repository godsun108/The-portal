Research date: 2026-10-11. This is source-based preliminary research, not legal advice or proof that any stream is playable.

## Primary publisher sources

- NASA media usage guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
- NASA brand and collaboration restrictions: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/
- NASA public media usage and partnerships: https://www.nasa.gov/collaborating-with-nasa/strategic-partnerships/
- NASA internet audio/video standard: https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/NASA/NASA-STD-2821

## Preliminary findings

1. NASA states that its own media generally is not subject to US copyright and may be used for educational/informational purposes, including websites, subject to guidelines. NASA should be acknowledged; do not imply endorsement.
2. NASA also warns that third-party copyrighted material can appear in NASA content; NASA cannot grant rights in that material.
3. NASA names NASA+, NASA YouTube, Twitch, and DailyMotion as official video outlets. This does **not** itself verify that a specific live stream may be embedded, restreamed, or used commercially.
4. NASA logos, insignia and identity marks are restricted; do not use them as PORTAL branding or imply affiliation.
