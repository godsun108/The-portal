## LATER — research-only lanes
### E. Botanical smoke blend / pre-roll
- Preserve candidate-ingredient list as research only, with **no formulation or ingestion/inhalation safety inference**.
- Prioritize legal classification, expert inhalation-toxicology review, age/shipping/ad rules, permissible manufacturing, tests and insurance.
- No recipes, human trials, sales, marketing or procurement until safety and lawful pathway proven.

### F. Hemp nutrition / culinary seasoning
- Favor **food-grade hemp seed, hulled seed, seed protein or seed oil** where actual ingredient use meets relevant conventional-food rules.
- Research culinary seasonings with conventional herbs and spices; assess retail category, ingredients, labels and compliant manufacturing.
- Maintain separate R&D on cannabis/hemp leaves, microgreens and flower as food: nutrition per serving, cannabinoids, food-use legality and safety evidence. Do NOT market whole-plant flower or CBD-infused seasoning as cleared food.
- Do not conflate industrial hemp cultivation status with approval of every plant part for consumption.

## Fast-action activation protocol
1. Evidence: written safe/legal intended-use path, lab/test plan, supplier specifications and actual vendor quotations.
2. Business: product specification, customer/price/channel, entity/IP/trademark review, insurance and full cash requirements.
3. Authorization: named decision maker explicitly approves capped spend and success/stop criteria.
4. Pilot: lawful controlled test with measurable customer preference/reorders and unit economics.
5. Scale only after results, never merely because financing becomes available.

## Standing weekly review
Check vendor replies; record each new fact/date and blocker; pick one zero-cost next action per priority venture; maintain financial readiness and make no commitments without authorization.

This is an operational research plan and does not establish approval, safety or legal status of any product.
