# BLIND//SPOT

A perception/navigation cabinet built around inverse attention. The safe route fades near the pointer and becomes clearer farther away, forcing the traveler to use peripheral information rather than stare directly at the path. Leaving the corridor records a contact; four contacts end the run.

Reach the goal to earn `blind-thread`. Finish in under 32 seconds with zero contacts for mastery artifact `peripheral-eye`. Failure records `attention-burn`.
