# VECTOR//BREAK

A seven-wave action cabinet. Move the signal, aim with the pointer, and automatically fire into moving destructible geometry. Destroying blocks without taking damage builds a score multiplier; impacts reset the combo.

Converged Portal physics bends block trajectories. Clearing seven waves grants `broken-vector`; a clean high-score clear with at least three lives and 1800 points grants `vector-core`. Failure records `vector-burn`.
