# Product Safety / Claims Register — 2026-10-10
Status is not a statement that a product is safe or legal.

| Concept | Main hazard or constraint | Evidence needed | Claims prohibited until substantiated |
|---|---|---|---|
| CBD seasoning | FDA's position: added CBD in food cannot be introduced into interstate commerce; CBD not approved as food additive | Regulatory counsel analysis for each relevant jurisdiction/commerce mode; do not put on checkout | "FDA approved", pain or disease treatment, unverified relaxation benefits |
| CBD-free hemp seed seasoning | Food-ingredient specifications, allergens/cross contact, food handling and labeling | Match actual seed ingredient/specifications to relevant FDA GRAS notices; safety plan and packaging | Any CBD or cannabinoid-content claim without evidence |
| Inhalable botanical pre-rolls | Smoke/combustion exposures, contamination, psychoactive botanicals, variable effects; legal classification and shipping | Qualified inhalation-toxicology assessment per ingredient AND finished-use profile; state/federal counsel and testing plan | "Safe to smoke", "healthy", "lung cleansing", "anxiety treatment", "helps quit smoking" |
| Hemp cannabinoid pre-rolls | Florida hemp extract requirements, testing/COA, product restrictions, age 21+ for inhalation or ingestion; other state and federal rules | Qualified local compliance review including sourcing, THC standards, labeling, age verification and shipping rules | "Legal everywhere", "FDA approved" |
| Cacao shell tea | Heavy metals (lead/cadmium), microbial safety, intended food use, possible caffeine/theobromine | Food-grade supplier COAs, origin/processing, use classification, label review | Disease treatment/detox claims |
| Sparkling tea and zero-proof drinks | Microbiological/process validation of canned/shelf-stable drinks and ingredient legality | Commercial formula, validated process and shelf life, label/claims review | "Shelf stable" without validation or unsupported functional claims |

## Research boundaries
- An herb's history of use or safety as tea does NOT establish inhalation safety.
- A supplier COA is necessary evidence for some hazards but is not an authorization to market or a complete toxicology assessment.
- Different states, ages, online shipping, and advertising platforms can impose independent restrictions.
- Avoid psychoactive/therapeutic positioning until qualified evidence and law support it.
- No smoke-blend recipes, ratios or human consumption trials in this phase.
