# PORTAL Continuous Universe

An original open-world science-fiction architecture spanning interiors, cities, wilderness, atmosphere, orbit, stations, deep space and planetary surfaces.

**First vertical slice:** apartment -> street -> vehicle -> spaceport -> spacecraft -> atmosphere -> orbit.

Prove one continuous playable journey before expanding galaxy breadth.

EIDOLON supplies persistent human/animal/vehicle actors. PORTAL owns interactive scene state, input, replication and gameplay. VHE/director systems supply characters, dialogue and cinematics. Narration can power radio, mission control, NPC speech and news.

Use original universe, factions, vehicles, creatures, terminology, art and music. Genre inspirations are references, not assets or lore to copy.
