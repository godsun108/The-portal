# Material Platforms, Test Data, and Product Passports
2026-10-12 | Experimental hypotheses; zero validated grades.

## Laboratory program — eight materials
1. Clean hemp bast fiber: fiber diameter/length, moisture, tensile, impurities, batch consistency.
2. Chemically/physically pretreated fiber or cellulose: composition, molecular mass, fiber performance, contamination.
3. Lignin binder candidate: purity, bonding, fire/VOC release, strength/aging, durability.
4. Mycelium-bound hurd: density, water uptake, dimensional change, compressive behavior, microbiology/inactivation, mold/odor, acoustic/fire testing.
5. Hemp reinforced biopolymer composites: verified biobased fraction, reinforcement wt%, interfacial behavior, impact/creep and recycling end-of-life.
6. Industrial biochar filler: particle size, ash/metals/PAHs, dispersion, mechanical and thermal changes, dust/exposure.
7. Graphene-like carbon: structural identity, layer distribution, defects and contaminants following appropriate techniques; independent graphene characterization per ISO/TS 21356-1. Don't claim all char is graphene.
8. Hybrids: hemp fiber + char/graphenic additive + proven resin, test whether performance/cost improves relative to incumbent fiber composites and carbon black.

## Product-passport standard record (one version per batch and application)
- Internal grade ID, intended part/customer standard, tested material formulation, binder and bio-based content.
- Chain of custody: hemp origin, fiber/char/mycelium lots, chemical additives, supplier COAs.
- Manufacturing parameters recorded by qualified partner, in-process QC and sample traceability.
- Third-party measured density, tensile, flexural, compression, impact, creep, moisture, UV/corrosion/thermal and fire behavior as applicable.
- Exposure/emissions: worker handling/dust and airborne particles; VOCs and odor.
- Electrical: conductivity/ESD/EMI/insulation with explicit trade-offs where appropriate.
- Aerospace space-specific: offgassing under applicable protocol, thermal vacuum/cycling, radiation, flammability, contamination and lot controls where applicable.
- Environmental: LCA boundary, durability/end-of-life, recyclability/compostability only where independently validated.
- Compliance: testing laboratory, accreditation, applicable version of standard, pass/fail and limitation, engineer/customer approval.
- Economics: per-part cost incl rejects, freight, tooling, line throughput and independent benchmark.
- Disposition: RESEARCH / ENGINEERING SAMPLE / QUALIFIED FOR SPECIFIC NONCRITICAL USE / CUSTOMER APPROVED / REGULATED APPLICATION QUALIFIED. Never write “certified” without documentary proof.

## Prototype project opportunities
- Mycelium/hemp packaging insert vs EPS benchmark.
- Biochar/hemp panel vs MDF and selected polymers.
- Fiber-reinforced molded robot housing vs incumbent ABS or glass-fiber composite.
- Conductive filler formulation benchmark: hemp-char vs carbon black vs independently qualified graphene.
- Durable marine interior part with moisture testing.
- Acoustic/decorative building panel under applicable fire rules.
These remain engineering sample proposals, not authorized human-use experiments or commercial production.

## Lab/industry partnership types
ISO-accredited materials test labs; polymer compounders; composite molders; accredited fire laboratories; academic materials characterization labs; building-code consultants; marine class and OEM labs; aerospace materials qualification specialists. Seek nonbinding information first; no equipment purchases or testing contracts without separate authorization.
