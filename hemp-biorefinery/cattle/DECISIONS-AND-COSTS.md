# Cattle Project — Critical Decisions, Financial and Legal Gates
As-of 2026-10-12. No cattle purchase, hemp ration or supplier commitment authorized.

## Decisions before livestock
1. Business objective: beef cow-calf, grass-fed finishing, dairy, conservation grazing or mixed? Each has different capital, labor and regulations.
2. Location and land rights: county/parcel land use, carrying capacity, water, floodplain/storm risk, proximity to a vet and stock facilities.
3. Cattle health and humane care: veterinarian, experienced farm partner, inspection/transport arrangements, vaccinations/parasite and reproductive care as advised.
4. Legally acceptable feed: standard forage/hay/grain/minerals first; hemp only after written specific regulatory confirmation, feed labeling and animal-product consumer safety review.
5. Market route: farm direct beef vs buyers, inspection and cold chain; dairy/milk licensing and refrigeration require substantially different operating systems.

## Safe acquisition decision
No animals before legal land use, functional fencing/water, full-time caretaker schedule, veterinary arrangement, emergency housing/transport, appropriate insurance, feed reserves and budget authorization.

## Hemp feed research outcomes
- Regulatory acceptance: unknown for cattle, must confirm with FDA/AAFCO and Florida feed regulators in writing.
- Nutritional effectiveness: preliminary controlled research, not commercial approval.
- Cannabinoid residue/consumer risk: demonstrable concern in leaves/spent biomass and some dairy studies.
- Therefore current operational ration: **conventional lawful, veterinary/nutritionist-reviewed feed only**.
No hemp feeding schedule, inclusion rate or homemade cannabinoid dosage proposed.
