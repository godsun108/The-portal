## No-spend readiness packet definition
Each venture should eventually contain:
1. One-page buyer/problem/occasion and product hypothesis.
2. Specific ingredient/product classification and dated source log.
3. Supplier identity and written specifications, not just marketplace prices.
4. 3 comparable products, quantities and recorded prices with observation dates.
5. Formula/manufacturing/QA requirements appropriate to format.
6. Budget worksheet separating one-time R&D, pilot inventory, variable COGS and retail/channel economics.
7. Safety/legality, margin and demand conditions to start a pilot.
8. Explicit budget approval and a maximum-at-risk amount.

## Response-processing protocol
When a vendor email arrives: read all attachments, log vendor assertions vs verified legal facts, note open questions, update cost table, and choose one consequential follow-up (avoid excessive outreach). Never copy confidential vendor quotations into public consumer marketing without permission.
