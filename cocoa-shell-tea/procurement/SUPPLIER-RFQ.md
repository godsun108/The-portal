# Cacao Shell Supplier RFQ
Please provide: (1) cacao species and ingredient designation, (2) food-grade / beverage-use representation and any usage restrictions, (3) origin and lot traceability, (4) processing/roasting procedure and sanitation, (5) representative and lot-specific COAs, including lead/cadmium, microbiology and any pesticides/mycotoxins screened, (6) allergen/cross-contact control, (7) moisture/water activity and shelf life, (8) package sizes/MOQs, (9) delivered sample and commercial pricing, (10) lead time, storage and handling, (11) recall/quality procedures, (12) whether selling the ingredient repackaged as a dry infusion is supported by your specifications.
