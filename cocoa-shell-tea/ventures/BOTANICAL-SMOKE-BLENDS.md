# Venture Concept — Botanical Smoking Blends / Pre-rolls
Status: RESEARCH ONLY / HIGHER-RISK GATED. Updated 2026-10-10.

## Concept
Pre-rolled botanical smoking products involving ingredients proposed by founder: mugwort, cacao shell, CBD, CBG, kanna, hyssop, skullcap and potential others. This ingredient list is an exploration queue ONLY, not a formula, and NONE of the listed materials is approved for inhalation based on inclusion here.

## Safety gate
Combustion and inhalation have material pulmonary risks even where an herb is edible or traditionally used in tea. Oral-use safety does not establish safe inhalation; inhaled doses, pyrolysis products, contamination and active pharmacology require separate assessment. Avoid unsubstantiated claims of safer smoking, relaxation, mood benefit, therapeutic effect or nicotine cessation. Particular botanicals may bring allergy, medication interaction, psychoactive or neurological concerns; retain individual scientific review.

## Compliance gate
Before procurement or sale, qualified counsel/regulatory review must determine intended product classification, local/state/federal permits, tobacco-free smoking product rules, combustible hemp restrictions, cannabinoid testing/THC thresholds, packaging, age verification, advertising, internet sales, shipping restrictions, platform policies and product-liability insurance. Florida's hemp extract statute imposes requirements including age 21+ for products containing hemp extract intended for inhalation; additional laws may apply. Source: https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/0581.217

## Development status
No blends, ratios, rolling procedures, manufacturer instructions or finished product authorized. Do not test on people or describe product as safe. Initial market discovery may cover adult customer needs, competitor claims, intended-use legal landscape, noninhalable alternatives and licensed qualified production/test vendors.
