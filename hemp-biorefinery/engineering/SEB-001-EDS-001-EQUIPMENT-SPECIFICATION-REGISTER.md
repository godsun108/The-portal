# SEB-001-EDS-001 — Equipment Specification Register

**Revision:** 0.1 | **Date:** 2026-11-26 | **Status:** Preliminary, vendor-neutral

## 1. Purpose
Register the equipment packages required for the 1 dry t/day initial module and future 5/10/25 dry t/day expansions. This is a functional register, not approved purchase specifications. No equipment sizes, materials, ratings, vendors, costs or guarantees are established without feed data, process design and vendor evidence.

## 3. Required datasheet fields
For each package: process duty; normal/minimum/maximum capacity; design margin; feed properties; operating/design pressure and temperature; materials of construction; corrosion/erosion; utility loads; controls/instrumentation; safeguards; emissions/waste; footprint/weight; access/maintenance; codes/certifications; vendor references; warranty; spares; delivery; installation/commissioning; performance test; exclusions; and lifecycle cost.

## 4. Vendor qualification and acceptance
Require documented reference installations, feed similarity, measured throughput, product quality and recovery, test methods, utility consumption, emissions/wastewater data, reliability, cleaning/plugging history, safety certifications, scope split, guarantees and remedies. Laboratory or pilot claims must be labeled as such and not presented as commercial guarantees. Any vendor test, paid study, sample shipment, NDA or purchase requires separate owner approval.

## 5. Procurement hold points
No RFQ release, paid vendor evaluation, sample testing, purchase order, deposit or contract is authorized by this register. Proceed first with nonbinding technical information gathering under the existing equipment RFQ research file.
