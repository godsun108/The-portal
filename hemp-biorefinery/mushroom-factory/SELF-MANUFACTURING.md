# The Self-Manufacturing Factory — Generational Engineering Roadmap
2026-10-12. Vision: increasing locally manufactured fraction of equipment, not total industrial self-replication.

## Stage 0 — conventional certified equipment, self-designed noncritical parts
Buy code-compliant motors, pumps, PLC controls, safety interlocks, bearings, pressure vessels, electrical switchgear and sanitary food-contact components. Design and fabricate noncritical tooling, jigs, protective machine guards after safety review, mold trays, removable panels, acoustic enclosures and non-load-bearing brackets where mechanical and fire tests permit.

## Stage 1 — hemp-based manufacturing materials
- Hemp-bast reinforced composites / fiberboard for tool housings, tables, protective skins, pallets and packaging after product qualification.
- Mycelium-bound hemp hurd for passive packing inserts, acoustic panel cores, decorative components, noncritical thermal insulators with appropriate fire/moisture test.
- Lignin-derived binders and biochar-filled polymer panels research, with VOC and combustion/worker exposure assessment.
- Machine design files, CAD models, bill of materials, validated material property database and repeatability/QC procedure.
Do not label hemp + plastic as 100% hemp or biodegradable when the binder prevents it.

## Stage 2 — manufacture selected machine components
Use certified fabrication facilities and material qualification to attempt casing panels, machine base skins, material handling containers, noncritical conveyor covers, replaceable abrasion inserts and molds. Design interfaces for purchased metal shafts, fasteners, sensors, pumps and drive systems.
Before accepting a part: tensile/flexural/compression strength, impact, creep, water/chemical resistance, dimensional stability, heat/fire, sanitation/contact suitability, electrical/flammability and fatigue where relevant. Failure mode and qualified engineer approval required.

## Stage 3 — circular tooling
Retire chipped or damaged polymer/fiber parts to validated reuse or recycling where actual polymer chemistry permits. Develop remanufacturable fixture/mold materials with product-specific lifecycle assessment. Make next-generation tooling with qualified internal raw materials; continue buying high-precision/safety-critical parts where required.

## Stage 4 — frontier R&D
Graphene-like carbon from qualified biomass-derived carbon may be evaluated as a composite filler, subject to independent layer structure/purity and finished-component performance. Not yet a guaranteed graphene manufacturing business. Fuel equipment, industrial electrical equipment, pressure-rated process hardware, rotating high-energy parts, reactor vessels and fire suppression systems remain certified external/engineered supply chains unless a separate licensed manufacturing capability and rigorous qualification are established.

## A quantifiable metric
Self-manufactured share = qualified in-house component production cost / complete equipment bill of materials, tracked by mass, criticality, lead time and failure consequences. **Never maximize this percentage at the expense of safety, cost or reliability.**
