# Workshop machine nature

A committed Workshop construction receives a deterministic nature from its actual 7×5 geometry. This is not random lore.

- **ANCHOR** — crosses the center cell and touches at least three outer-edge cells.
- **BEACON** — touches at least four outer-edge cells.
- **BRIDGE** — its occupied bounding box is at least as wide as it is tall.
- **SEED** — all other valid committed constructions.

Nature is derived on read and does not overwrite the original construction record.
