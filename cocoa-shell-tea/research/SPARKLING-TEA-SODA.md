# Track B — Sparkling Tea Soda Replacement
**Stage:** idea / product brief; unpriced and unvalidated.
**Customer job:** everyday refreshing soft drink with satisfying carbonation and enjoyable flavor, real tea identity, and a sugar profile customers accept.
**Initial SKUs under consideration:** peach black tea; citrus green tea; cherry hibiscus tea. Candidate flavors only, not recipes.
**Target decisions:** 12 oz vs 8 oz can; caffeinated vs decaf; 0g added sugar vs lightly sweetened; natural flavors vs brewed tea percentage; sweetener strategy; carbonation intensity; target retail 4-pack or single; cold-chain vs validated shelf-stable.
**Research queue:** category benchmark of 5+ SKUs and price/oz; blinded sweetness/flavor preference questions; sourcing estimates for teas, acids, flavors, cans/ends/trays; co-packer MOQ and formula ownership; processing and shelf-life regulatory classification.
**Bench sensory protocol:** select three comparable safe food-grade formulas, record ingredient weights & concentrations, blind-test chilled servings versus soda comparators for overall liking (1–9), sweetness appropriateness, carbonation, tea perception and purchase intent; record all feedback, not claims.
**Gate to pilot:** formula specification, validated processing requirement, costed small production and compliant labeling reviewed. Carbonation alone is NOT proof of shelf stability.
