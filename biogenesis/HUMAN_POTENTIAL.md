# BioGenesis Human Potential — research charter v0.1

## Mission
Study evidence for improving human health, function, recovery, and resilience across the lifespan. Distinguish treatment of disease or deficiency from enhancement of healthy individuals. No claims of superhuman performance, invulnerability, or guaranteed longevity.

## Research domains
- Musculoskeletal: strength, bone density, recovery, and injury prevention.
- Cardiometabolic: endurance, aerobic capacity, energy metabolism.
- Neurocognitive: attention, learning, memory, neuroprotection.
- Regeneration: tissue healing and restoration of function.
- Healthy aging: functional lifespan, mobility, age-associated disease prevention.
- Resilience: immune function, environmental stress adaptation, and safety.

## Evidence review schema
Every research record should state:
1. Population: healthy volunteers vs a specific condition, age, and baseline characteristics.
2. Intervention class: behavior, approved therapy, experimental medicine, or speculative mechanism.
3. Comparator: placebo, standard care, or established intervention.
4. Outcomes: validated clinical or functional endpoints, not solely molecular markers.
5. Evidence tier: mechanistic, preclinical, observational, randomized clinical, or systematic review.
6. Safety: known adverse effects, contraindications, uncertainty, and follow-up duration.
7. Source: traceable primary publication or authoritative review, with date.
8. Interpretation: measured benefit, null result, mixed evidence, or untested hypothesis.

## Non-negotiable boundaries
- No unapproved drug self-experimentation, dosing, compounding instructions, or autonomous synthesis.
- No optimizing venom toxins or other hazardous agents for biological potency.
- Molecular docking and simulations cannot establish human efficacy or safety.
- Human clinical use requires qualified oversight, ethics review when applicable, and relevant regulatory authorization.
- Never imply a discovery, clinical benefit, or Genesis learning improvement without reproducible supporting evidence.

## Implementation phases
1. Evidence-based Academy module and human-potential research taxonomy.
2. Curated literature records with citations, outcome measures, and confidence levels.
3. Safe, read-only visualization of known structures and physiological pathways.
4. Benchmarked computation, with clear uncertainty and limitations.
5. Regulated laboratory collaboration only after governance, funding, and qualified partners.

## Current implementation status
This document is a research charter. It does not imply an active medicinal chemistry model, a validated clinical prediction system, or an automated pharmacy. No spending, deployment, or Victory/VictoryChain access is authorized.
